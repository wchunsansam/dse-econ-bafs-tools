5.1A 可變生產要素和固定生產要素Variable factors and fixed factors

生產要素可按它會否隨產量的增減而變動，分為可變生產要素和固定生產要素。

Factors of production can be classified into variable factors and fixed factors by whether their quantities vary with output.

可變生產要素是指數量會隨產量的增減而變動的投入。可變成本是僱用可變生產要素的成本，它會隨產量的增減而變動。

Variable factors are inputs whose quantities vary with output. Variable costs are the costs of employing variable factors. They vary with output.

如果產量是零，廠商便毋須僱用可變生產要素，所以可變成本也是零。

If output is zero, no variable factors are employed and variable costs are zero.

固定生產要素是指數量不會隨產量的增減而變動的投入。固定成本是僱用固定生產要素的成本，它不會隨產量的增減而變動。

Fixed factors are inputs whose quantities do not vary with output. Fixed costs are the costs of employing fixed factors. They do not vary with output.

即使產量是零，廠商仍須僱用固定生產要素（除非廠商結業），所以固定成本是正數。

Even if output is zero, fixed factors may still be employed, unless the business closes down, and fixed costs are positive.

生產要素Factor 成本Cost
會隨產量增減而變動Varies with output 可變生產要素Variable factor 可變成本Variable cost
不會隨產量增減而變動Does not vary with output 固定生產要素Fixed factor 固定成本Fixed cost

錢包製造商（課本 p.4）：工具數量一直是 2 單位，成本 $15,000，是固定生產要素和固定成本。皮革由 0 單位增至 38 單位，成本由 $0 增至 $38,000，是可變生產要素和可變成本。產量是零時，皮革成本是 $0，但仍然要買工具，固定成本大於 $0。

A wallet manufacturer (Textbook p. 4): tools stay at 2 units and cost $15,000. They are a fixed factor and a fixed cost. Leather rises from 0 units to 38 units and its cost rises from $0 to $38,000. It is a variable factor and a variable cost. When output is zero, the leather cost is $0, but the tools are still bought, so fixed cost is greater than $0.

機器不一定是固定生產要素，勞力也不一定是可變生產要素。要看該項投入的數量是否隨產量增減而變動。

Machinery is not necessarily a fixed factor, and labour is not necessarily a variable factor. Check whether that input’s quantity varies with output.

請茶延長營業：小麗把舖延至深夜。電費和兼職時薪隨多開的時段上升，是可變成本。舖租和小明月薪不隨這一小時的杯數改變，是固定成本。

Try Tea stays open later: Eslyn keeps the shop open into the night. The electricity fee and the hourly wage of part-time staff rise with the extra hours. They are variable costs. The shop rent and Bob’s monthly salary do not change with the cups sold in that extra hour. They are fixed costs.

5.1B 短期和長期Short run and long run

按生產是否涉及固定生產要素，生產時期可分為短期和長期。

There are two production runs: the short run and the long run. They are classified by whether fixed factors exist.

在短期，至少有一個生產要素是固定生產要素，其餘是可變生產要素。

The short run is the period over which at least one factor of production is a fixed factor and the other factors are variable factors.

在長期，所有生產要素都是可變生產要素，沒有固定生產要素。

The long run is the period over which all factors of production are variable factors. There are no fixed factors.

固定 + 可變Fixed + variable → 短期Short run
只有可變Variable factors only → 長期Long run

應試攻略 5.1：生產時期不是按時間長短來劃分

Exam Tips 5.1: Production runs are not classified by the length of the time period

看有沒有固定生產要素

Look for a fixed factor

不論時間多長，只要至少有一個固定生產要素，生產就是短期。

No matter how long the time period is, production is in the short run if there is at least one fixed factor.

不同行業調校全部要素所需的時間不同

Industries need different lengths of time to adjust every factor

便利店開新分店和聘新人，可能要兩個月。電力公司興建新電廠和擴展電網，可能要數年。兩段都是該行業的長期，因為所有生產要素都可以變動。

A convenience store may take two months to open a new branch and hire new staff. An electricity company may take several years to build a new power plant and extend the network. Both periods are the long run for that industry, because every factor can be varied.

5.2A 產量的量度Measurement of output

在短期，固定生產要素的數量不會隨產量增減而變動，所以我們量度不同數量的可變生產要素的產量。

In the short run, the quantity of fixed factors does not change with output, so output is measured at different quantities of the variable factor.

總產量（TP）是在指定期間內，某數量的可變生產要素所生產的產出數量。

Total product (TP) is the amount of output produced by a given quantity of a variable factor during a specified period.

平均產量（AP）是在指定的期間內，平均每單位可變生產要素的產量。

Average product (AP) is the output per unit of a variable factor on average during a specified period.

AP = 總產量 ÷ 可變生產要素的數量Total product ÷ units of the variable factor

邊際產量（MP）是在指定的期間內，額外僱用一單位可變生產要素所引致的總產量變動。

Marginal product (MP) is the change in total product from employing an extra unit of a variable factor during a specified period.

MPn = TPn − TPn−1

ABC 汽車廠（課本 p.6–7）：機器是固定生產要素，勞力是可變生產要素，以工時計算。

ABC Car Factory (Textbook p. 6–7): machinery is the fixed factor and labour, measured in man-hours, is the variable factor.

2 單位勞力的總產量是 22 單位汽車。平均產量 = 22 ÷ 2 = 11。第 3 單位勞力的邊際產量 = 36 − 22 = 14。

The total product of 2 units of labour is 22 cars. Average product = 22 ÷ 2 = 11. The marginal product of the 3rd unit of labour = 36 − 22 = 14.

邊際產量可以是負數。當 MP < 0，總產量下降。

Marginal product can be negative. When MP < 0, total product is decreasing.

只得平均產量時，先乘回總產量，再算邊際產量。只得邊際產量時，先加出總產量，再算平均產量。

If only average product is given, find total product first, then marginal product. If only marginal product is given, find total product first, then average product.

5.2B 邊際回報遞減定律Law of diminishing marginal returns

在短期，廠商不能透過不斷增加可變生產要素無限制地增加總產量。

In the short run, a firm cannot increase its total product without limit by increasing the variable factor.

邊際回報遞減定律指出，假設科技水平不變，在短期，當更多的可變生產要素不斷地加到某定額的固定生產要素時，該可變生產要素的邊際產量最終會下降。這定律又稱為邊際產量遞減定律。

The law of diminishing marginal returns states that in the short run, when more units of a variable factor are continuously added to a given quantity of fixed factors, the marginal product of the variable factor will eventually decrease, holding technology constant. It is also called the law of diminishing marginal product.

ABC 汽車廠（課本 p.10）：機器維持 2 單位。勞力的邊際產量是 10、12、14、13、12、11。僱用第 4 單位勞力時，邊際產量由 14 降至 13，定律在這裡開始適用。也可以說：僱用第 3 單位勞力後，邊際產量開始下降。

ABC Car Factory (Textbook p. 10): machinery stays at 2 units. The marginal product of labour is 10, 12, 14, 13, 12, 11. When the 4th unit of labour is employed, marginal product falls from 14 to 13. The law sets in there. We can also say marginal product starts to decrease after the 3rd unit of labour.

定律只說邊際產量最終會下降，不一定一開始就下降。最初上升，可能是因為固定生產要素尚未用盡，多加可變生產要素可以更充分運用它。

The law only says marginal product will eventually decrease. It need not fall from the first unit. An early rise can happen because the fixed factor is not yet fully used. More of the variable factor can use it more fully.

最終必定下降，是因為固定生產要素的容量有限。額外的可變生產要素沒有足夠的固定生產要素配合。所以這定律只適用於短期。

It must eventually fall because the fixed factor has a limited capacity. Extra units of the variable factor do not have enough of the fixed factor to work with. That is why the law applies only in the short run.

邊際產量開始下降時，總產量和平均產量仍可以上升。上例第 4 單位：邊際產量由 14 降至 13，但總產量由 36 增至 49，平均產量由 12 升至 12.25。不要把平均產量下降當成邊際產量下降。

When marginal product starts to fall, total product and average product can still rise. At the 4th unit above, marginal product falls from 14 to 13, but total product rises from 36 to 49 and average product rises from 12 to 12.25. Do not treat a fall in average product as a fall in marginal product.

請茶的同一間舖：舖是固定生產要素。小麗先請小明，再請小新，再請小美。最初每人多開一張枱，邊際產量可以上升。舖的座位用盡之後，再加人只會互相阻擋，邊際產量最終下降。換一間更大的舖並同時加人，就不是這條定律，因為舖也不再固定。

The same Try Tea shop: the shop is the fixed factor. Eslyn first hires Bob, then Sam, then Karen. At first each extra person opens another table, so marginal product can rise. Once the seats are full, extra people get in one another’s way and marginal product eventually falls. Moving to a larger shop and hiring more people at the same time is not this law, because the shop is no longer fixed.

例題拆解 5.1　數據是否說明邊際回報遞減定律Guided Example 5.1　Whether the data illustrate the law

課本 p.12Textbook p. 12

寫出邊際回報遞減定律，並解釋下列數據是否說明該定律。

State the law of diminishing marginal returns and explain whether the following data illustrate the law.

（a）甲廠：資本全部是 2 單位。勞力 1 至 5 單位，平均產量依次是 15、16、17、17、16。

(a) Factory A: capital is 2 units throughout. Labour is 1 to 5 units, and average product is 15, 16, 17, 17, 16.

（b）乙廠：資本是 2、4、6、8、10 單位，勞力是 1、2、3、4、5 單位，平均產量依次是 30、32、34、32、30。

(b) Factory B: capital is 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and labour is 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Average product is 30, 32, 34, 32, 30.

解題思路

Thinking process

先問是不是短期。若所有生產要素都在變，是長期，定律不適用。若是短期而題目只給平均產量，先算總產量，再算邊際產量。邊際產量隨可變生產要素增加而下降，定律才適用。

First ask whether production is in the short run. If every factor is changing, it is the long run and the law does not apply. If it is the short run and only average product is given, find total product, then marginal product. The law applies when marginal product falls as the variable factor increases.

答案：定律的內容見上面的定義。

Answer: the statement of the law is the definition above.

（a）適用。總產量是 15、32、51、68、80。邊際產量是 15、17、19、17、12。僱用第 4 單位勞力時，邊際產量由 19 降至 17。

(a) Yes. Total product is 15, 32, 51, 68, 80. Marginal product is 15, 17, 19, 17, 12. When the 4th unit of labour is employed, marginal product falls from 19 to 17.

（b）不適用。資本和勞力都在變動，是長期。平均產量下降不是邊際產量下降。

(b) No. Both capital and labour vary, so it is the long run. A fall in average product is not a fall in marginal product.

某廠商只僱用機器和勞力。機器全部是 10 單位。勞力 1、2、3、4 單位的平均產量是 10、11、12、X。若數據說明邊際回報遞減定律，X 的值

B. 必定是 12

D. 必定小於 12

A firm employs only machinery and labour. Machinery is 10 units throughout. Average product of 1, 2, 3 and 4 units of labour is 10, 11, 12 and X. If the data illustrate the law of diminishing marginal returns, the value of X
A. can be 12.5
B. must be 12
C. can be larger than 12
D. must be smaller than 12

解題思路

Thinking process

總產量 = 平均產量 × 勞力單位。第 4 單位的邊際產量必須小於第 3 單位的邊際產量。

Total product = average product × units of labour. The marginal product of the 4th unit must be smaller than the marginal product of the 3rd unit.

答案：C。總產量是 10、22、36、4X。邊際產量是 10、12、14、4X − 36。4X − 36 < 14，所以 X < 12.5。12.2 大於 12，又小於 12.5，所以 X 可以大於 12。12.5 本身不會使邊際產量下降。

Answer: C. Total product is 10, 22, 36, 4X. Marginal product is 10, 12, 14, 4X − 36. Then 4X − 36 < 14, so X < 12.5. A value such as 12.2 is larger than 12 and still below 12.5, so X can be larger than 12. X = 12.5 itself does not make marginal product fall.

5.3 成本的量度Measurement of costs

總成本（TC）是在指定的期間內，生產某數量產出的成本。總成本 = 總可變成本 + 總固定成本。

Total cost (TC) is the cost of producing a given quantity of output during a specified period. Total cost = total variable cost + total fixed cost.

平均成本（AC）是在指定的期間內，平均生產一單位產出的成本。平均成本 = 總成本 ÷ 產量。

Average cost (AC) is the cost of producing a unit of output on average during a specified period. Average cost = total cost ÷ output.

邊際成本（MC）是在指定的期間內，額外生產一單位產出所引致的總成本變動。MCn = TCn − TCn−1。邊際成本的詳細討論見第 9 章。

Marginal cost (MC) is the change in total cost of producing an extra unit of output during a specified period. MCn = TCn − TCn−1. The fuller treatment of marginal cost is in Chapter 9.

MAX 電池廠（課本 p.17）：生產 2 單位時，總可變成本 $180，總固定成本 $120，總成本 = 180 + 120 = $300。平均成本 = 300 ÷ 2 = $150。第 3 單位的總成本是 $360，邊際成本 = 360 − 300 = $60。

MAX Battery Factory (Textbook p. 17): at 2 units, total variable cost is $180 and total fixed cost is $120, so total cost = 180 + 120 = $300. Average cost = 300 ÷ 2 = $150. Total cost of the 3rd unit’s output is $360, so marginal cost = 360 − 300 = $60.

在長期沒有固定生產要素，總固定成本是 0。

In the long run there is no fixed factor, so total fixed cost is 0.

Look at total cost when output is zero

5.4A 擴大生產規模的結果Results of increasing the scale of production

在長期，所有生產要素都是可變的。廠商增加所有生產要素的數量時，生產規模便會擴大。總成本會上升，但長期平均成本可以上升或下降。

In the long run, all factors of production are variable. When a firm increases its employment of all factors, its scale of production increases. Total cost rises, but long run average cost may rise or fall.

長期平均成本（LRAC）是在長期，平均生產一單位產出的成本。LRAC = 總成本 ÷ 產量。

Long run average cost (LRAC) is the cost of producing a unit of output on average in the long run. LRAC = total cost ÷ output.

規模經濟是指擴大生產規模的好處，會令長期平均生產成本下降。

Economies of scale are the advantages of a larger scale of production that lower long run average cost.

規模 ↑ → 產量 ↑ → LRAC ↓

Scale ↑ → output ↑ → LRAC ↓

規模負經濟是指擴大生產規模的壞處，會令長期平均生產成本上升。

Diseconomies of scale are the disadvantages of a larger scale of production that raise long run average cost.

規模 ↑ → 產量 ↑ → LRAC ↑

Scale ↑ → output ↑ → LRAC ↑

廠商不斷擴大生產規模時，通常最初出現規模經濟，最終出現規模負經濟。長期平均成本曲線呈 U 形。曲線的最低點是最優規模。

When a firm keeps expanding, it normally faces economies of scale at first and diseconomies of scale eventually. The LRAC curve is U-shaped. The lowest point is the optimal scale.

規模經濟，LRAC ↓Economies of scale, LRAC ↓ → 最優規模，LRAC 最低Optimal scale, LRAC at a minimum → 規模負經濟，LRAC ↑Diseconomies of scale, LRAC ↑

生產規模超過最優規模，廠商不一定虧損。最優規模是平均成本最低，不是利润必定為正或必定為負的那一點。

A firm producing beyond the optimal scale is not necessarily making a loss. The optimal scale is the lowest average cost. It is not the point where profit must be positive or must be negative.

規模經濟和規模負經濟是長期概念。廠商必須沒有固定生產要素，才用這兩個概念。

Economies and diseconomies of scale are long run ideas. The firm must have no fixed factor before these ideas are used.

比較總成本和產量的變動百分比：產量上升的百分比大於總成本上升的百分比，長期平均成本下降，是規模經濟。產量上升的百分比小於總成本上升的百分比，長期平均成本上升，是規模負經濟。

Compare the percentage changes. If the percentage rise in output is greater than the percentage rise in total cost, long run average cost falls: economies of scale. If the percentage rise in output is smaller than the percentage rise in total cost, long run average cost rises: diseconomies of scale.

長期數據（課本 p.21）：資本和勞力一起增加。產量 100、200、300、400、500，平均成本 $6、$5、$4、$5、$6。由 100 至 300，平均成本下降，是規模經濟。300 單位是最優規模。由 300 至 500，平均成本上升，是規模負經濟。

Long run data (Textbook p. 21): capital and labour increase together. Output is 100, 200, 300, 400, 500 and average cost is $6, $5, $4, $5, $6. From 100 to 300, average cost falls: economies of scale. 300 units is the optimal scale. From 300 to 500, average cost rises: diseconomies of scale.

5.4B 規模經濟和規模負經濟的來源Sources of economies and diseconomies of scale

來源分兩類：廠商自己擴大（內部），以及整個行業擴大（外部）。課程不要求背來源的名稱，要能按情境解釋長期平均成本為何下降或上升。

Sources are of two kinds: the firm itself expands (internal), or the whole industry expands (external). The names of the sources are not required. What is required is an explanation, in the given situation, of why long run average cost falls or rises.

內部Internal

內部規模經濟是廠商擴大自己的生產規模時享有的好處，令它的長期平均成本下降。

Internal economies of scale are advantages a firm enjoys when it enlarges its own scale of production, so that its long run average cost falls.

  • 員工之間可以有更精細的分工，管理效率和生產力上升。
  • A more extensive division of labour raises managerial efficiency and productivity.
  • 較大的廠商可提供較多抵押品，能以較低利率向銀行借款。
  • A larger firm can offer more collateral and may borrow at a lower interest rate.
  • 大量購買原材料，可以獲得較大折扣。
  • Buying raw materials in bulk can bring a larger discount.
  • 市場推廣成本，以及研究和發展成本，可以攤分到較大產量。
  • Marketing costs, and research and development costs, can be spread over a larger output.
  • 機器和設備可以更充分運用，生產力上升。
  • Machines and equipment can be used more fully, so productivity rises.

內部規模負經濟是廠商把自己的生產規模擴大至超過最優規模時面對的壞處，令長期平均成本上升。

Internal diseconomies of scale are disadvantages a firm suffers when it enlarges its own scale beyond the optimal scale, so that long run average cost rises.

  • 架構過於複雜，協調變弱，決策變慢，管理效率下降。
  • The organisation becomes too complicated, coordination weakens, decisions slow down, and managerial efficiency falls.
  • 若已有大量未償還貸款，再借款時可能要付較高利率。
  • If the firm already has large outstanding loans, further loans may require a higher interest rate.
  • 所需投入供應有限時，可能要以較高價格購買，或採用較昂貴的替代品。
  • When the supply of necessary inputs is limited, the firm may buy more expensive inputs or use more expensive substitutes.
  • 市場飽和時，推廣產品的市場推廣成本上升。
  • When the market is saturated, marketing costs of promoting the product rise.

生活實例 5.1　WeWork 過度擴張Living Case 5.1　Overexpansion of WeWork

課本 p.25Textbook p. 25

WeWork 把辦公室分成較小的共用空間再租出。2019 年它在 110 個城市經營，是美國最大的初創公司之一。2023 年因過度擴張和管理不善申請破產。

WeWork divides office premises into smaller co-working spaces and lets them out. In 2019 it operated in 110 cities and was one of the biggest startups in the US. In 2023 it filed for bankruptcy after overexpansion and poor management.

擴張初期可能享有的內部規模經濟：大量購買辦公用品和傢俬可獲折扣，亦可與業主議得較低租金；市場推廣成本，以及預約系統和會員系統的開發成本，可以攤分到更多共用空間。

Internal economies it may have enjoyed at first: discounts on office supplies and furniture bought in bulk, and lower rents negotiated with landlords; marketing costs, and the cost of developing the booking and membership systems, spread over more co-working spaces.

過度擴張後可能面對的內部規模負經濟：累積 29 億美元債務，再借款可能要付較高利率；共用空間市場可能飽和，推廣成本上升。

Internal diseconomies it may have faced after overexpanding: US$2.9 billion of debt, so further loans may cost a higher interest rate; the co-working market may be saturated, so marketing costs rise.

外部External

外部規模經濟是整個行業擴大時，個別廠商享有的好處，令它的長期平均成本下降。

External economies of scale are advantages an individual firm enjoys when the industry expands, so that its long run average cost falls.

  • 更多工人受相關訓練，招聘有經驗工人的成本下降。
  • More workers are trained for the industry, so the cost of recruiting experienced workers falls.
  • 行業更知名，吸引更多顧客，個別廠商的市場推廣成本下降。
  • The industry becomes better known and attracts more buyers, so an individual firm’s marketing costs fall.
  • 維修、運輸、貯存等支援服務更多，採購成本下降。
  • More back-up services, such as repair, transport and storage, lower the cost of sourcing.
  • 行業集中在一個地區時，交通網絡可能改善，運輸成本下降。
  • If the industry clusters in one area, the transport network may improve and transport costs fall.

外部規模負經濟是整個行業擴張過度時，個別廠商面對的壞處，令它的長期平均成本上升。

External diseconomies of scale are disadvantages an individual firm suffers when the industry expands beyond the optimal scale, so that its long run average cost rises.

  • 勞力、廠房和原材料的需求大增，工資、租金和原料價格上升。
  • Demand for labour, factories and raw materials rises sharply, so wages, rent and raw-material prices rise.
  • 市場飽和，個別廠商的市場推廣成本上升。
  • The market may be saturated, so an individual firm’s marketing costs rise.
  • 支援服務的產能有限，服務價格上升，採購成本上升。
  • Back-up services have limited capacity, their prices rise, and the cost of sourcing rises.

(b) Answer: external diseconomies of scale. The demand for trained markers and invigilators rises, so the cost of recruiting experienced workers rises. The mock-exam market may be saturated, so marketing costs rise.

請茶在同一間舖多請小美，邊際產量下降。小麗說這是規模負經濟。這句哪裡錯了？

Try Tea hires Karen in the same shop and marginal product falls. Eslyn calls this diseconomies of scale. What is wrong?

答案：舖沒有變，是短期。邊際產量下降是邊際回報遞減定律。規模負經濟要所有生產要素都可以變動，而且是長期平均成本上升。

Answer: the shop is unchanged, so this is the short run. The fall in marginal product is the law of diminishing marginal returns. Diseconomies of scale need every factor to be variable, and they are a rise in long run average cost.
