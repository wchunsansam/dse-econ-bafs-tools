10.1 貨幣政策的意思Monetary policy

貨幣政策是指中央銀行透過控制貨幣供應（Ms）和／或名義及實質利率，以達到某些經濟目標的政策。

Monetary policy refers to the measures taken by the government or the central bank to achieve certain economic goals by adjusting money supply or interest rate.

這些目標包括穩定一般物價水平、刺激或減慢經濟增長、降低失業率等。課堂假設貨幣供應由中央銀行決定，貨幣供應曲線是垂直線：無論利率怎樣改變，貨幣供應量都維持不變。

Similar to fiscal policy, these goals include reducing the unemployment rate and cooling down an overheated economy. Money supply is set by the monetary authority, so the Ms curve is vertical.

貨幣政策主要包括兩種：

Monetary policy is mainly divided into two types:

擴張性貨幣政策

Expansionary monetary policy

透過增加貨幣供應和／或調低利率，刺激經濟。

The policy to raise the money supply or lower interest rate.

它是擴張性的，因為短期可以提高總產出。

It is expansionary because it can raise aggregate output in the short run.

緊縮性貨幣政策

Contractionary monetary policy

透過減少貨幣供應和／或調高利率，壓抑過熱經濟。

The policy to lower the money supply or raise interest rate.

它是緊縮性的，因為短期可以降低總產出。

It is contractionary because it can lower aggregate output in the short run.

擴張性貨幣政策如何影響名義利率與總需求How expansionary policy affects the nominal interest rate and AD

當貨幣供應增加，名義利率會下降。

When there is an increase in money supply, the nominal interest rate will decrease.

名義利率下降令外資流走，對本地貨幣需求減少，本地貨幣價值下跌（貶值），導致本地出口貨品價格（以外幣計算）下降，令出口淨值上升。

When the nominal interest rate decreases, foreign investment will flow out of the economy to earn a higher interest rate. The value of the domestic currency decreases, causing the price of domestic products relative to foreign products to decrease. This leads to an increase in net exports.

結果：貨幣供應增加會令總需求增加。

As a result, an increase in money supply will lead to an increase in aggregate demand.

短期對物價水平與總產出Short-run effects on the price level and aggregate output

擴張性貨幣政策令總需求增加，因此短期物價水平與總產出都會上升。

An expansionary monetary policy leads to an increase in aggregate demand. Therefore in the short run, the price level and aggregate output would increase.

結論：它只能在短期令經濟擴張至超越充分就業水平（Yf）。垂直的長期總供應曲線意味着該經濟體系在長期必會停留於充分就業水平。

It can raise output above full employment only in the short run. A vertical long-run aggregate supply curve means the economy stays at the full-employment level in the long run.

緊縮性貨幣政策Contractionary monetary policy

當貨幣供應減少，名義利率會上升。借貸成本上升 → C、I ↓；外資流入 → 本幣升值 → (X−M) ↓。結果總需求減少（AD 左移）。短期物價水平與總產出都會下降。

When there is a decrease in money supply, the nominal interest rate will increase. The cost of borrowing increases → C and I fall. Foreign investment flows in, the domestic currency rises, and net exports fall. Aggregate demand decreases. In the short run, the price level and aggregate output would decrease.

應試攻略：先分清貨幣政策還是財政政策

Exam tip: first separate monetary policy from fiscal policy

正確：公開市場操作、貼現率、法定儲備比率、印鈔＝貨幣政策。易錯：把加稅、轉移支付、基建、部委津貼寫成貨幣政策——那些是財政。

Correct: open-market operations, the discount rate, the required reserve ratio and printing money are monetary. Trap: calling a tax rise, a transfer, infrastructure spending or a ministry cash handout monetary policy — those are fiscal.

澄茶兼職與減息Chloe’s part-time job and a rate cut

樂澄在屯門澄茶兼職。若中央銀行增加貨幣供應、名義利率下降，店主借錢開分店的成本下降，消費與投資都會增加，總需求上升。短期店裏加班多了（產出升），珍珠和杯蓋也貴了（物價升）。這是課堂故事，不是把財政部發消費券當成貨幣政策。

Chloe works part-time at Ching Cha in Tuen Mun. If the central bank raises money supply and the nominal interest rate falls, the owner’s cost of borrowing to open a branch falls, so consumption and investment rise and AD rises. In the short run she works more overtime (output up) and tapioca costs more (prices up). A government consumption voucher is fiscal, not monetary.

10.2 貨幣政策工具Monetary tools

中央銀行主要以下列工具來改變貨幣供應，以帶來擴張或緊縮效果：公開市場操作、貼現率、法定儲備比率、印發鈔票。

The government (or central bank) can use various monetary tools to affect the money supply to bring about an expansionary or contractionary effect: open market operations, the discount rate, the required reserve ratio, and printing money.

公開市場操作Open market operations

公開市場操作指中央銀行在公開市場買賣政府債券（或其他政府證券）。

Open market operations refers to the central bank’s buying and selling of government bonds in the market.

貼現率Discount rate

貼現率是中央銀行向其他銀行收取的貸款利率。

Discount rate refers to the interest rate that the central bank charges on its loans to commercial banks.

若貼現率上升，商業銀行向中央銀行借貸的機會成本上升，它們貸出超額儲備的意欲下降，可創造存款減少及貨幣供應減少。若貼現率下降，則相反：借貸成本下降，貸出意欲上升，可創造存款與 Ms 增加。

Lowering the discount rate: commercial banks can borrow from the central bank at lower cost, borrow more and loan out more; through deposit creation, money supply increases. Raising the discount rate does the reverse through deposit contraction.

應試攻略：貼現率先寫「向央行借貸成本」

Exam tip: start from the cost of borrowing from the central bank

正確：貼現率 ↑ → 向央行借貸成本 ↑ → 銀行少借儲備、少貸款 → 存款收縮 → Ms ↓。易錯：跳過儲備／存款創造，直接寫「市場利率一定立刻上升」。

Correct: a higher discount rate raises the cost of borrowing from the central bank, so banks borrow less reserves and lend less; deposit contraction cuts Ms. Trap: skipping reserves / deposit creation and jumping straight to “the market interest rate must rise at once”.

法定儲備比率Required reserve ratio

法定儲備比率是銀行須把存款中的最低比例以儲備形式持有。

Required reserve ratio is the minimum fraction of deposits that banks are required to keep as reserve.

若法定儲備比率上升，可貸出的超額儲備減少、可創造存款減少及貨幣供應減少。若下降，則超額儲備增加、可創造存款增加及 Ms 增加。

Lowering the required reserve ratio: banks have more excess reserves to loan out; through deposit creation, money supply increases. Raising it leaves banks with less excess reserves, or they even call back loans; through deposit contraction, money supply decreases.

調法定儲備比率不改變貨幣基數，只改最大銀行乘數。不要把「一間商業銀行向另一間商業銀行購買政府債券」寫成會增加體系貨幣供應——體系儲備不變。

Changing the required reserve ratio does not change the monetary base; it changes the bank multiplier. A commercial bank buying government bonds from another commercial bank does not raise system-wide money supply — system reserves are unchanged.

印發鈔票Printing money

中央銀行可印發鈔票以買入政府債券，令流通貨幣與銀行存款增加，貨幣供應增加；名義利率下降；C、I ↑ → AD ↑ → 產出上升，屬擴張性。

The central bank may print money to buy government bonds so that currency in circulation and bank deposits increase. Money supply increases, the nominal interest rate decreases, C and I rise, AD and output rise: expansionary.

10.3 以貨幣政策處理宏觀問題Monetary policy as a tool to tackle macroeconomic problems

結構題固定四步：指出合適政策 → 說明如何執行 → 貨幣市場效果 → 物價與產出效果（標 E1→E2、P1→P2、Y1→Y2／Yf）。

Structured questions use four steps: identify the suitable policy → how it is carried out → effects on the money market → effects on the price level and aggregate output (label E1→E2, P1→P2, Y1→Y2 / YF).

失業（通縮缺口）The case of unemployment (deflationary gap)

存在失業時，短期均衡實質國民收入（Y1）低於充分就業實質國民收入（Yf）。擴張性貨幣政策所引致的總需求增加（Ms↑→名義利率↓→ C & I↑→ AD↑）可在短期提高產出水平和物價水平（分別由 Y1 升至 Y2 以及由 P1 升至 P2）。當產出水平回復至充分就業水平時，市場在長期便不會有進一步的調整。

Unemployment is caused by an excess supply of workers: the short-run equilibrium output level is below the full-employment output level. To close the deflationary gap, use expansionary monetary policy (e.g. buying government bonds, lowering the discount rate, lowering the required reserve ratio or printing money). Higher Ms lowers the nominal interest rate and raises the quantity of money. Lower borrowing costs raise C and I. AD shifts from AD1 to AD2; equilibrium from E1 to E2; P1 to P2; Y1 to Y2 (YF), reducing unemployment.

Y1 < YfY1 < YF → 擴張性 Ms↑Expansionary Ms↑ → i↓ → C、I↑i↓ → C, I↑ → AD↑；Y→YfAD↑; Y→YF

經濟過熱（通脹缺口）The case of overheating (inflationary gap)

經濟過熱時，Y1 大於 Yf。緊縮性貨幣政策所帶來的總需求減少（Ms↓→名義利率↑→ C & I↓→ AD↓）在短期會降低產出水平和物價水平（由 Y1 降至 Y2、P1 降至 P2）。當產出水平回復至充分就業水平，市場在長期便不會有進一步的調整。

Overheating occurs when production capacity cannot keep pace with growing demand: short-run equilibrium output is above full-employment output. To close the inflationary gap, use contractionary monetary policy (sell bonds, raise the discount rate or raise the required reserve ratio). Lower Ms raises the nominal interest rate and cuts the quantity of money. Higher borrowing costs cut C and I. AD shifts from AD1 to AD2; E1 to E2; P1 to P2; Y1 to Y2 (YF).

應試攻略：長期不能改變實質產出

Exam tip: real GDP is unchanged in the long run

正確：垂直 LRAS 停在充分就業（潛在產出）；AD 右移只推高物價。易錯：畫一條向上斜的 LRAS，或寫「長期產出繼續上升」。

Correct: a vertical LRAS stays at potential output; a rightward AD shift only raises the price level. Trap: an upward-sloping LRAS, or writing that output keeps rising in the long run.

飯堂加價與過熱Canteen prices and overheating

梓柔發現學校飯堂和補習社都在加價，工廠加班，Y 已高於 Yf。這是通脹缺口：應用緊縮性貨幣政策（例如賣債或提高貼現率），而不是再印鈔刺激。

Tsz Yau finds the school canteen and her tutorial centre both raising prices, and factories on overtime, so Y is already above YF. That is an inflationary gap: use contractionary monetary policy (sell bonds or raise the discount rate), not more money-printing.

小練習 10.3C　長期實質產出Practice 10.3.1 Q2　Real GDP in the long run

試以總供應—總需求圖輔助，解釋為何擴張性貨幣政策在長期不能影響實質 GDP。

With the aid of an AS–AD diagram, explain why an expansionary monetary policy cannot affect real GDP in the long run.

解題思路

Thinking process

先寫利率與 C、I，再寫 AD 右移；長期鎖定在潛在產出，只加價。圖必須有垂直 LRAS。

Write the interest-rate channel to C and I first, then the AD shift. Lock long-run output at potential output; only the price level rises. The diagram must have a vertical LRAS.

答案要點：擴張性貨幣政策會降低利率（1）；消費和投資會增加（1）；總需求增加（總需求曲線右移）（1）；然而長期總產出停留在潛在產出（1）；總需求增加只會導致物價水平上升（1）。正確圖：垂直的長期總供應曲線停在潛在產出（1）；總需求曲線右移（1）；總產出維持不變（1）。

Answer: Expansionary monetary policy will lower the interest rate. (1) Consumption and investment will increase. (1) Aggregate demand increases (AD curve shifts to the right). 10.5 貨幣數量論Quantity theory of money

貨幣數量論主要探討一個經濟體系內的「貨幣數量」對「一般物價水平」和「產出」的影響。貨幣數量是指名義貨幣供應；建基於交易方程式這概念。（N.B. 假設貨幣流速是一常數，即不變。）

Inflation and the quantity theory of money: velocity of circulation of money is assumed to be constant. The QTM proposes a positive relationship between nominal money supply and the long-run price level. It is built on the equation of exchange.

交易方程式Equation of exchange

最終產品的總開支 ≡ 最終產品的總值，即 MV ≡ PY。P 是物價水平；Y 是實質本地生產總值（或實質國民收入）；M 是貨幣數量（名義貨幣供應）；V 是貨幣流通速度。

The equation of exchange focuses on money as a medium of exchange. It describes the relationship between total expenditure of consumers and total receipt of producers: MV ≡ PY.

貨幣流通速度是指某段時間內，每一單位貨幣流通的速度，即每 $1 轉手的次數。V = PY / M，可視為名義本地生產總值佔貨幣數量的比率。

Velocity of circulation of money (V) is the average number of times a unit of money is used in a period of time to buy goods and services. It is equal to the ratio of nominal output (PY) to nominal money supply (M): V = PY / M.

交易方程式是一條恒等式：用於購買最終產品的貨幣總值（MV）必定等於所購買終端產品的總值（PY）。貨幣數量論不是恒等式，也不等於交易方程式。

The equation of exchange is an identity. The quantity theory of money is not identical to the equation of exchange, and the QTM is not an identity.

應試攻略：只知 M 與名義產出時只能肯定 V

Exam tip: given only M and nominal output, you can be sure about V

正確：若 M 與 PY 以同一比率增長，可斷定 V 不變。易錯：同時斷定物價上升或實質產出不變——沒有分開 P 與 Y 的資料就不能這樣寫。

Correct: if money supply grows at the same rate as nominal income, velocity is constant. Trap: also concluding that the price level is rising or real output is constant — you cannot split P from Y with that information alone.

貨幣數量論的定義與假設Definition and assumptions of the QTM

貨幣數量論是解釋通貨膨脹的理論，建基於 MV ≡ PY，並假設貨幣流通速度頗為穩定。

The Quantity Theory of Money (QTM) proposes a positive relationship between nominal money supply and the long-run price level. It is a theory which explains inflation.

貨幣流通速度由消費模式、交易技術與交易頻率等因素決定；這些因素甚少改變，故假設 V 不變。實質國民收入在短期可能會變動，但在長期則會維持於充分就業的水平。

Velocity is determined by consumption patterns, technology of transactions and transaction frequency. As these factors seldom change, velocity is assumed to be constant.

應試攻略：「V 不變」是假設，不是推論

Exam tip: constant V is an assumption, not a prediction

正確：貨幣數量論假設流通速度由支付制度、經濟結構和技術等因素決定，基本上不變。易錯：寫成「貨幣數量論推斷貨幣流通速度不變」。

Correct: constant velocity is an assumption of the QTM. Trap: writing that the QTM predicts that velocity is constant.

推斷：百分變動Predictions: percentage changes

由於 MV ≡ PY 是恒等式，左方轉變的百分率必定等於右方。假設 V 固定，名義貨幣供應的轉變必定為名義國民收入帶來同比例轉變。因此貨幣數量（M）與名義國民收入（PY）成正向關係：%ΔM = %ΔP + %ΔY（當 %ΔV = 0）。

Percentage change in the components: as the equation of exchange is an identity, the change on both sides should be the same. Since velocity is assumed constant, %ΔM = %ΔP + %ΔY. An increase in money supply also implies an equal percentage increase in nominal output (PY).

短期貨幣數量論

Short run

假設：Ms 由央行決定；%ΔV = 0。物價水平（P）和實質國民收入（Y）都是可變的。推論：%ΔM = %ΔP + %ΔY；%ΔM = %Δ(PY)。當 M 改變，P 和／或 Y 會隨之改變。

In the short run, both the price level and real output are variable. An increase (decrease) in money supply may lead to an increase (decrease) in the price level and/or real output.

長期／古典貨幣數量論

Long run / classical QTM

再假設經濟處於充分就業，實質產出不變（%ΔY = 0）。推論：%ΔM = %ΔP。P 與 M 作同方向、同比例轉變。

In the long run, real output remains constant at the full-employment level, therefore an increase (decrease) in money supply leads to an equal percentage increase (decrease) in the price level.

經濟直觀：「太多貨幣追逐太少物品」——Ms 增加時人們把額外貨幣花在物品與服務上，需求上升；長期實質產出不變，需求上升推高價格。

Economic reasoning — “too much money chasing too few goods”: when money supply increases, people spend the extra money on goods and services. Demand rises. In the long run real output is constant, so the increase in demand drives up prices.
