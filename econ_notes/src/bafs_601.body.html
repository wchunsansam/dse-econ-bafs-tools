2　市場營銷的定義The marketing concept

重點在於 顧客（顧客導向）。企業應了解顧客的真實需要和慾望，並按他們的需要提供最合適的產品和服務（即顧客導向）以賺取利潤，並比競爭對手更能更有效率地滿足目標市場。

Focus on the Customer (Customer-oriented). A business should find out the needs of the customers (target markets) and try to satisfy those needs in order to earn profit and deliver the desired satisfactions more effectively and efficiently than competitors.

請茶改賣少糖Try Tea cuts the sugar

小美說學生不想再喝全糖。請茶先問清楚他們要少糖還是要茶味濃，再改配方。這是先看顧客，不是先把倉庫裡的糖漿用完。

Karen says students no longer want full sugar. Try Tea asks whether they want less sugar or a stronger tea taste, then changes the recipe. That starts from the customer, not from using up the syrup already in the store.

社會市場營銷概念Societal marketing concept

企業不單要滿足目標市場的需要，也要維護甚至提升整體的利益。

A company not only needs to focus on corporate profits and customer needs, but also on the well-being of society.

4.1　檢討目前的表現Reviewing current performance

目的：評估其產品目前在目標市場中的位置。檢討市場營銷表現及財務表現來進行評估。可以將本身的表現與主要競爭對手進行比較。

To assess the current competitive position of a company’s products in their existing target markets. Through review of its marketing and financial performance. By comparing its performance measures against those of its major competitors.

評估市場表現：銷貨收益、市場佔有率。評估財務表現：投資回報、現金流量、利潤。

Common measures of reviewing marketing performance: sales revenue, market shares. Common measures of reviewing financial performance: return on investment, cash flow, profit margin.

英文原文寫 “To access”。這裡改成 assess（評估）。中文財務那一項是「利潤」；英文是 profit margin。市場表現的英文是 market shares。

The photocopy prints “To access”. This note uses assess. The Chinese financial item is 利潤; the English item is profit margin. The English marketing measure is market shares.

4.2　進行強弱機危分析Conducting SWOT analysis

目的：仔細分析企業現時面對的外在環境及內在情況，幫助企業訂立市場目標。

Analyze the internal and external environment of the company. help firms set marketing objective.

課本練習：P. 31 Q17(a)。

Textbook exercise: P. 31 Q17(a).

內在Internal

例子：供應商、顧客、競爭是外在例子，不要放在這一格。內在例子：財務資源、企業形象、地點、生產設備, 研究與開發。

e.g. financial resources, company image, research & development, location, and production facilities

外在External

地方的經濟, 政治, 科技, 法治, 社會及文化環境等等

e.g. micro-environmental factors: suppliers, marketing intermediaries, customers, competitors & public

macro-environmental factors: demographic, economic, physical, technological, political, legal, social & cultural forces

正面Positive 負面Negative
內在Internal

Strengths 強項：有助企業達成目標的內在優勢。酒店：房外景觀優美。麥當勞：商譽良好，市場佔有率高。

Strengths: Internal capabilities that may help a company reach its objectives. Hotel: Luxurious rooms. (McDonald’s) extensive distribution, high brand recognition, average price.

Weaknesses 弱項：可能阻礙企業達成目標的內在因素。酒店：地理位置: 距離機場較遠。麥當勞：食品較不健康。

Weaknesses: Internal limitations that may adversely affect a company’s ability to achieve its objectives. Hotel: distant from airport. (McDonald’s) unhealthy products, difficult to implement revolution ∵ large scale.
外在External

Opportunities 機會：對企業有利的外在因素。酒店：奧運舉辦。麥當勞：新興市場（中國, 中東等）。

Opportunities: External factors that the company may enrich its competitive advantage. Hotel: Olympic Games. (McDonald’s) Emerging markets (China, India, Middle East).

Threats 危機：目前和可能出現，或會影響企業表現的外在不利因素。酒店：通脹。麥當勞：人民開始著重健康問題。

Threats: Current and emerging external factors that may challenge the company’s performance. Hotel: Inflation. (McDonald’s) The rising public awareness of being healthy & pursuing nutritious food.

中文酒店威脅寫「通漲」，這裡改成「通脹」。中文機會的新興市場是中國、中東；英文多了 India。中文弱項的麥當勞只寫食品較不健康；英文還有 difficult to implement revolution、large scale。中文強項的麥當勞是商譽和市場佔有率；英文是 extensive distribution、high brand recognition、average price。外在例子的清單兩邊用字不同，各留各的。

The Chinese hotel threat prints 通漲; this note uses 通脹. Chinese emerging markets are China and the Middle East; English adds India. The Chinese McDonald’s weakness is only the unhealthy food; English also has the revolution and large-scale limits. The Chinese McDonald’s strength is reputation and market share; English is distribution, brand recognition and average price. Keep each language’s external-factor list.

請茶的四格Try Tea in four boxes

小麗的店在學校門口，下課人流多，這是內在優勢。雪櫃舊了，這是內在因素。附近新開一間便利店賣瓶裝茶，這是外在不利因素。學校禁止含糖飲品的消息如果還沒實行，也可以是機會：請茶可以先改無糖。

Eslyn’s shop is at the school gate, so after-school traffic is an internal capability. An old fridge is an internal limitation. A new convenience store selling bottled tea is a current external factor. A school rule against sugary drinks, before it starts, can also be an external factor the shop can use by switching to unsweetened tea.

4.3　訂立市場營銷目標 (SMART)Setting marketing objectives (SMART)

引導企業進行市場營銷活動及成為監控市場營銷活動的準則。

Setting objectives guide the marketing operations and provide a standard for controlling purposes.

  • 市場表現：下一年銷貨收益增加１０％
  • Marketing performance: increasing sales revenue by 10% over the next year
  • 財務表現：下一年利潤增加１０％
  • Financial performance: increasing net profits by 15% over the next year

中文財務目標是下一年利潤增加 10%。英文是 increasing net profits by 15% over the next year。不要把兩個百分比改成同一個。

The Chinese financial target is a 10% rise in profit next year. The English line is increasing net profits by 15% over the next year. Keep the two percentages apart.

4.4　研究及選定目標市場Researching and selecting target markets

例子：百佳、Citysuper、ThreeSixty。

Examples: PARKnSHOP, Citysuper, ThreeSixty.

  1. 評估每一個細分市場（市場組別）的吸引力。市場組別是指一組擁有共同特徵的顧客。
  2. To assess the attractiveness of each market segment. A marketing segment is a sub-group of customers who share similar patterns of buying behaviour.
  3. 選取最適合的細分市場（又稱「市場組別」）作目標市場。
  4. To select the most promising segment (i.e., target market) to serve.

中文紅字是「吸引力」「共同特徵」「目標市場」。英文沒有把 attractiveness 畫線，畫的是 sub-group、similar patterns、promising、target market。

Chinese marks 吸引力, 共同特徵 and 目標市場. English does not mark attractiveness. It marks sub-group, similar patterns, promising and target market.

Objective: to maintain and enhance the company’s competitive position. Textbook P. 17. That objective sentence is not marked in English. Chinese marks 鞏固 and 提升.

7　監控市場營銷活動Controlling marketing activities

監控企業的市場營銷活動，確保活動能依照計劃順利進行。如實際表現不能達到可接受的範圍，便應採取行動作出糾正。

Monitoring activities to ensure that they are being carried out as planned. Taking necessary actions to correct all the identified significant deviations.

四步的案例標籤：[麥當勞]。英文是 [case: fast food chain store]。

The Chinese case label is 麥當勞. English marks [case: fast food chain store].

  1. 訂立表現標準。[在一年內設定了銷售目標, 例上升20%]

    Set performance or target standards. [set sales target within one year]

  2. 量度實際的市場營銷表現。[量度那一年的銷售額]

    Measure actual marketing performance. [measure sales target within that year]

  3. 比較實際表現與標準。分析到底實際銷售表現能不能達到可接受的差異範圍。（實際銷量: 增加5%）

    Compare the actual marketing performance against the target standard. [determine if the actual sales performance falls within the acceptable range of variation (e.g., + 5% as predetermined by the fast food chain store)]

  4. 差異很大？[如果超出可接受範圍……] 第四步 (a) 採取糾正行動。第四步 (b) 不採取行動。為未來計劃。

    Is the deviation significant? [If the variation identified in step 3 falls beyond the acceptable range of variation, the fast food chain store should…] Step 4(a) take corrective actions. Step 4(b) take no action. Input for future planning.

Step 1 to Step 4 are underlined headings. The blanks are the red examples in brackets, plus Monitoring, planned and correct in the definition.
