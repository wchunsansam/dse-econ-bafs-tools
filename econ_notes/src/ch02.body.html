2.1 三個基本經濟問題The three basic economic problems

由於存在稀少性，我們必須決定如何把稀少資源用來滿足無窮的慾望。所有關於資源分配和物品分配的決定，都牽涉三個基本經濟問題。

In the face of scarcity, we need to decide how to use scarce resources to satisfy unlimited wants. All decisions on the allocation of resources and the distribution of goods involve the three basic economic problems.

稀少性Scarcity
引致causes
三個基本經濟問題Three basic economic problems
A 生產甚麼？What to produce? 類型 + 數量Types + quantities
B 如何生產？How to produce? 生產方法Production methods
C 為誰生產？For whom to produce? 分配準則Distribution criteria

A. 生產甚麼？What to produce?

「生產甚麼」問題是指選擇物品和服務的類型和數量。

The problem of ‘what to produce’ refers to the choice of the types and quantities of goods and services to produce.

面對稀少性，有限資源只能滿足部分慾望，所以必須決定生產哪些物品和服務、生產多少。

Facing scarcity, we can only use limited resources to satisfy some wants. We have to decide what and how many goods and services to produce.

課堂例子：請茶要賣甚麼Class example: what Try Tea should sell

樂澄在請茶兼職。店舖地方細，不能每樣都做：多做珍珠奶茶，就要少做檸檬茶。這是「生產甚麼」——選類型（珍奶還是檸檬茶）和數量（各做多少杯）。同一塊地若只夠建球場或醫院其中一樣，也是「生產甚麼」。

Chloe works at Try Tea. The shop is small, so more bubble tea means less lemon tea. That is ‘what to produce’: the type (bubble tea or lemon tea) and the quantity. The same logic applies if a parcel of land is only big enough for a stadium or a hospital.

B. 如何生產？How to produce?

「如何生產」問題（也稱「怎樣生產」）是指選擇生產方法。

The problem of ‘how to produce’ (also ‘in what way to produce’) refers to the choice of production methods.

面對稀少性，會選成本最低的方法，以生產盡量多的物品和服務。這包括：

Because of scarcity, the least costly production method is chosen to produce as many goods and services as possible. This involves the choices of:

  • 生產要素（例如勞力和資本；詳見第 4 章）
  • factors of production (e.g. labour and capital; see Chapter 4)
  • 生產時間和地點（例如在本地還是海外生產）
  • production time and venues (e.g. produce locally or overseas)
  • 生產策略／技術（例如分工、勞力密集或資本密集）
  • production strategies / techniques (e.g. division of labour, labour intensive or capital intensive)
課堂例子：請茶怎樣沖茶Class example: how Try Tea makes drinks

請茶可請多兩個兼職（勞力密集），或買自動封杯機（資本密集）。政府用外勞起樓、蘋果把組裝外判到印度，都是「如何生產」——改的是方法，不是賣甚麼、賣給誰。

Try Tea can hire two more part-timers (labour intensive) or buy an automatic sealer (capital intensive). Importing construction workers, or assembling iPhones in India rather than the US, is also ‘how to produce’: the method changes, not what is sold or who may buy it.

C. 為誰生產？For whom to produce?

「為誰生產」問題是指選擇決定誰可享受物品和服務的準則。

The problem of ‘for whom to produce’ refers to the choice of criteria for who can enjoy the goods and services.

由於稀少性，現有物品和服務不能滿足所有人的慾望，必須訂立準則來分配。準則可以是產品價格、人們的收入、生產者的偏好、投票等。

Because of scarcity, existing goods and services cannot satisfy everyone’s wants, so criteria are needed. Criteria include product prices, people’s income, producers’ preference, voting, etc.

課堂例子：誰喝得到最後兩杯Class example: who gets the last two cups

放學後請茶只剩兩杯。可以用價格（誰付得起加價）、先到先得（誰先排隊）、或只賣給本校學生。準則一改，能喝到的人就不同——這是「為誰生產」。

After school Try Tea has two cups left. It can use price (who will pay more), first-come first-served (who queues), or ‘students of this school only’. Change the criterion and a different person gets the drink — that is ‘for whom to produce’.

書上的分配準則例子：名牌手袋定價 $100,000（價格）；政府拍賣土地給最高出價者（出價）；房委會為公屋訂入息及資產限額（由 2025 年 4 月 1 日起，二人家庭每月入息上限 $20,230）。

Textbook criteria: a luxury handbag priced at $100,000 (price); government land sold to the highest bidder; the Hong Kong Housing Authority’s income and asset limits for public rental housing (from 1 April 2025, the monthly income limit for a two-person family is $20,230).

應試攻略 2.1：有些情況涉及多於一個基本經濟問題

Exam Tips 2.1: Some cases involve more than one economic problem

先在題幹標三類字

Mark three kinds of wording in the stem

類型／數量 → 生產甚麼。方法、地點、用誰、用甚麼機器 → 如何生產。誰合資格、誰可享受、限額、免費給誰 → 為誰生產。

Types / quantities → what. Method, venue, who is employed, which machine → how. Who is eligible, who may enjoy, a quota, who gets it free → for whom.

沒提及的問題不要硬加

Do not add a problem the stem never mentions

書例：慈善基金會撥款為甲國農村 18 歲以下女孩興建學校。興建學校 = 生產甚麼；18 歲以下、住在該區的女生 = 為誰生產。計劃沒有提及生產方法，故不涉及「如何生產」。

Book case: a charity foundation announces a donation to build a school for girls under 18 in a rural area of Country A. Building a school = what to produce; girls under 18 in that area = for whom. The proposal does not mention the production method, so ‘how to produce’ is not involved.

  • 正確：只圈題幹寫到的那些問題。
  • Correct: tick only the problems the stem actually states.
  • 易錯：看見「興建」就自動加「如何生產」，或看見「學校」就當三題全中。
  • Trap: adding ‘how’ just because something is built, or ticking all three whenever you see a school.

小練習 2.1AQuick check 2.1A

題 1Q1

請茶決定本週少做檸檬茶、多做珍珠奶茶。這是哪一個基本經濟問題？

Try Tea decides to make fewer lemon teas and more bubble teas this week. Which basic economic problem is this?

答案：生產甚麼

Answer: what to produce

改的是物品的類型和數量，不是沖茶方法，也不是誰可以買。

The type and quantity of drinks change, not the method or who may buy.

題 2Q2

學校飯堂改用自動打飯機，少請一個兼職。這是哪一個基本經濟問題？

The canteen installs a rice machine and hires one fewer part-timer. Which basic economic problem is this?

答案：如何生產

Answer: how to produce

改的是生產方法（資本代替勞力），不是賣甚麼飯、也不是誰可打飯。

The production method changes (capital instead of labour), not the menu or who may eat.

題 3Q3

巴士公司計劃把車費加 9%。這是哪一個基本經濟問題？

A bus firm plans to raise fares by 9%. Which basic economic problem is this?

答案：為誰生產

Answer: for whom to produce

改的是誰付得起、誰可乘車的準則（價格），不是開哪條線、也不是用哪種巴士。

The fare changes who can afford to ride (the criterion), not which route or which type of bus.

題 4Q4

放學後校門派免費報紙，先到先得。這是哪一個基本經濟問題？

Free newspapers are handed out at the school gate, first-come first-served. Which basic economic problem is this?

答案：為誰生產

Answer: for whom to produce

準則決定誰可拿到報紙。先到先得是分配準則，不是生產方法。

The criterion decides who may have a copy. First-come first-served is a distribution rule, not a production method.

2.2 社會如何解決基本經濟問題How society tackles the basic economic problems

A. 解決經濟問題的方法Methods of tackling economic problems

社會可用三種方法解決三個基本經濟問題。同一社會往往不止用一種。

Society can tackle the three problems in three ways. Most societies do not use only one method.

① 通過風俗和慣例By customs and traditions

人們可按風俗和慣例來解決經濟問題。

Economic problems can be tackled by customs and traditions.

② 通過政府指令By government command

政府有時在資源分配和物品分配上擔當重要角色。

Sometimes the government plays an important role in deciding the allocation of resources and the distribution of goods and services.

  • 生產甚麼：房委會計劃在 2023/24 至 2027/28 年度興建至少 11 萬個公屋單位；醫管局計劃在啟德發展區興建急症醫院。
  • What: the Housing Authority plans at least 110,000 public rental flats from 2023/24 to 2027/28; the Hospital Authority plans an acute hospital in the Kai Tak Development Area.
  • 如何生產：填海造人工島（明日大嶼）；政府收費隧道改用「易通行」不停車繳費。
  • How: reclaiming land for artificial islands (Lantau Tomorrow); replacing booths with HKeToll at government-tolled tunnels.
  • 為誰生產：二元優惠合資格年齡降至 60 歲；綜援設入息及資產限額。
  • For whom: the $2 Scheme eligible age was lowered to 60; CSSA has income and asset limits.

③ 通過市場機制By market mechanism

在市場機制（也稱價格制度，詳見第 10 章）下，市場價格是引導資源分配和物品分配的信號。生產者和消費者按市場價格作決定。

Under the market mechanism (also the price system; see Chapter 10), the market price is the signal that guides the allocation of resources and the distribution of goods and services. Producers and consumers decide according to market prices.

無形之手The invisible hand

亞當·斯密（Adam Smith, 1723–1790）提出：在市場經濟中，個人追求自身利益，可以在沒有中央操控下使社會整體得益。書上把這隻「無形之手」放在本章開首。

Adam Smith (1723–1790) argued that in a market economy, people pursuing their own interest can benefit society as a whole without central control. The textbook places this ‘invisible hand’ on the chapter opener.

  • 生產甚麼：更多人喜歡在咖啡店見面 → 市場上咖啡店增加；人們更注重健康 → 廠商生產更多健康和有機食品。
  • What: more people meet in cafes → more cafes; people become more health-conscious → more healthy and organic food.
  • 如何生產：餐廳用機械人送餐；Google Health 用人工智能做醫療診斷。
  • How: restaurants use robots for delivery; Google Health used AI in medical diagnostics.
  • 為誰生產：付得起價格的人才得到智能手機；達文西《救世主》2017 年拍賣逾 4.5 億美元。
  • For whom: only those who can afford the price get a smartphone; Salvator Mundi sold for over US$450 million in 2017.

B. 經濟體系的類型Types of economies

按主要用來解決三個基本經濟問題的方法，經濟體系（有時簡稱經濟）可分為三類。表內反覆出現「主要」——不是「全部」。

Economies (sometimes just called an economy) are classified by the method they mainly use — not the only method they ever use.

不要自創「第四類」去取代書上的三類

Do not replace the three types with a fourth official class

書上按主要方法分成傳統、計劃、市場。實際上，大多數社會都不會只運用一種方法。民生必需、難有盈利的項目常有政府指令；其他物品多用市場價格。國情 360 把中國內地稱為混合經濟模式（社會主義市場經濟），那是說明「兩種方法並用」，不是多了一個與書表平行的第四格。

The book classifies economies by the method they mainly use: traditional, planned, market. In practice, most societies do not use only one method. Necessities that are hard to run at a profit often go by government command; many other goods go by market price. China 360 calls the mainland a mixed economy (a socialist market economy) to show both methods at work — not to add a fourth official box beside the table.

  • 正確：香港主要是市場經濟，但仍有公屋、公立醫院等政府指令。
  • Correct: Hong Kong is mainly a market economy, but public rental housing and public hospitals still use government command.
  • 易錯：寫「現今只有混合經濟，所以不用分三類」或「轉型為市場就一定更有效率／一定貧富懸殊」。
  • Trap: writing that only mixed economies exist so the three types do not matter, or that a switch to the market must raise efficiency or widen the income gap.

計劃經濟的特點是大部分資源由政府分配，不是「大部分生產活動由政府官員親手做」。市場經濟裡政府仍可規管商業活動；也不是所有資源的私有產權都獲清晰界定。經濟表現和收入分配與經濟體系類型沒有必然關係——不要寫「轉型為市場就一定貧富懸殊／失業上升／不再有稀少性」。

A planned economy means most resources are allocated by the government, not that officials themselves carry out most production. In a market economy the government may still regulate business; private property rights of all resources need not be well-defined. Economic performance and income distribution are not necessarily tied to the type of economy — do not write that a switch to the market must widen the income gap, raise unemployment, or end scarcity.

國情 360　中國內地的經濟體系China 360　The economy of the mainland of China

課本 p.27Textbook p. 28

現時中國內地奉行混合經濟模式（稱為社會主義市場經濟）。以公有制為主體，同時容許私營企業；法律界定和保障私有產權，鼓勵市場交易。不少商品由市場價格分配，政府也通過指令引導資源，例如樓價過高時限制地產商借貸、調整貸款利率。轉型前政府發糧票等供應票證分配資源；其後引入價格機制，由市場供需決定商品價格。

The mainland of China currently operates as a mixed economy (a socialist market economy). Public ownership is the mainstay, while private enterprises are allowed; the law defines and protects private property rights and encourages market exchange. Many goods are allocated by market prices, but the government also guides allocation by command — for example restricting developers’ borrowing and adjusting loan rates when property prices are high. Before the transition, ration coupons (grain coupons) allocated resources; later the price mechanism let supply and demand set prices.

2.3 專門化、交易和私有產權Specialisation, exchange and private property rights

市場機制是最常用來解決基本經濟問題的方式。它牽涉三個重要概念：專門化、交易和私有產權。

Market mechanism is the most common way to tackle the basic economic problems. Three ideas sit behind it: specialisation, exchange and private property rights.

A. 專門化Specialisation

專門化是指不同的人或經濟體系集中生產不同物品，或集中從事某物品的不同生產工序。

Specialisation occurs when different people or economies focus on producing different goods or different production stages of a good.

在指定數量的資源下，如人們或經濟體系按各自的優勢專門生產某些物品，整體產量就會增加。

With a given amount of resources, by specialising according to people’s or economies’ advantages, total output will increase.

課堂例子Class example

請茶：樂澄專心調茶，文諾專心收錢和抹枱——同一店內按工序專門化。書例：馬爾代夫專門從事旅遊業，沙特阿拉伯專門生產石油；高科技電子產品由不同人士負責不同工序。

At Try Tea, Chloe only makes drinks while Marcus only takes payment and wipes tables — specialisation by stage. The book: the Maldives specialises in tourism, Saudi Arabia in oil; high-tech electronics are split into stages done by different people.

B. 交易Exchange

交易是指買與賣的活動。它可在使用或不使用貨幣的情況下進行。

Exchange refers to the activity of buying and selling. It takes place with or without the use of money.

在不使用貨幣的情況下，直接以物品或服務進行的交易，稱為以物易物。

The direct exchange of goods or services without the use of money is called barter.

課堂例子：筆記換珍奶Class example: notes for bubble tea

家明用經濟科筆記跟欣怡換兩杯請茶——沒有貨幣，仍是交易（以物易物）。書上的漫畫也是筆記換兩罐可樂。

Ka Ming swaps his economics notes for two Try Tea drinks from Yan — still exchange, without money (barter). The book’s cartoon is notes for two cans of coke.

C. 私有產權Private property rights

私有產權是指對私有財產的獨有使用權、收入專屬權及轉讓權。

Private property rights refer to the exclusive right to use, the exclusive right to receive income from, and the right to transfer a private property.

私有財產是個人或私人廠商所擁有的物品。完整的私有產權包括以下三項：

Private property is a good owned by individuals or private firms. A complete set includes:

權利Right 意思Meaning
獨有使用權Exclusive right to use 擁有者有權排除他人使用該物品。不用它（放在衣櫃）也是使用權的一部分。The owner may exclude others. Leaving it unused (in a wardrobe) still counts.
收入專屬權Exclusive right to receive income 只有擁有者有權獲取該物品所產生的收入（出租、借出換午餐等）。Only the owner may receive income from it (rent it out, lend it for a lunch).
轉讓權Right to transfer 不論有償還是無償，擁有者有權把該物品轉讓予他所指定的任何人。The owner can transfer the good to anyone, with or without compensation.
書例：家誠的的士Book example: Peter’s taxi

未經許可無人可駕駛（獨有使用）；自己開或租給其他司機賺錢（收入專屬）；可出售或送贈（轉讓）。

No one may drive it without permission (use); he earns by driving it or renting it out (income); he may sell or give it away (transfer).

買了名牌手袋，擁有的是這個手袋的三權，不是設計師的設計。不能因為買了手袋就去生產一個設計近似的手袋。先租後買、實名門票、不可轉讓周遊券，都會削弱其中一項或多項權利——考試常問「被削弱」，不是「完全沒有產權」。

Buying a designer bag gives the three rights over that bag, not over the design. Hire-before-purchase, real-name tickets and a non-transferable rail pass weaken one or more rights — the exam often asks ‘weakened’, not ‘no property rights at all’.

D. 專門化、交易和私有產權之間的關係The relationship between specialisation, exchange and private property rights

私有產權的存在，是交易的先決條件。

The existence of private property rights is a precondition for exchange.

如物品或資源的私有產權獲清晰界定並得到保障，願意和有能力的人便會支付市場價格來獲取它們。這促成自願交易。沒有清晰產權（例如不清楚房子誰屬），人們不願意付錢購買。

When private property rights are well-defined and protected, people who are willing and able pay the market price. That enables voluntary exchange. If rights are not well-defined (no one knows who owns the house), people are not willing to pay.

交易是專門化的先決條件。

Exchange is a precondition for specialisation.

有交易，人們便毋須自行生產一切所需。他們可按優勢專門生產，再與他人交換。書例：蘋果農用蘋果換食物、衣服和藥物，因而可以專門種蘋果。

With exchange, people no longer need to produce everything themselves. They specialise in what they do better and exchange. Book example: an apple farmer exchanges apples for food, clothing and medicine, so he can specialise in growing apples.

清晰界定的私有產權讓交易和專門化得以實行，整體產量增加，生活水平提高。由於在市場經濟下私有產權至關重要，政府必須界定和保障私有產權。

Well-defined private property rights make exchange and specialisation possible, so total output and living standards rise. Because they matter in a market economy, the government must define and protect them.

公共財產（進階學堂 2.1）

Common property (Go Further 2.1)

沒有私有產權

No private property rights

公共財產是沒有私有產權的物品或資源。任何人都可以使用；沒有人擁有獨有使用權或收入專屬權，所以沒有人願意付費，也不會有市場交易。

Common property is a good or resource without private property rights. Everyone may use it. With no exclusive right to use or to receive income, no one would pay, so there is no market exchange.

價格制度無法運作時

When the price system cannot function

可能出現其他準則（非價格方法）：政府指令、先到先得，甚至暴力／戰爭。人們總想獲得更多，最終可能過度使用公共財產（野生動物被過度捕獵、雨林被過度開發）。

Other criteria may emerge: government command, first-come first-served, even violence / war. Users try to get the most, so common property may be overused (wildlife overhunted; rainforests over-exploited).

Snow mountains are natural resources over which no one has private property rights, so the water there is not private property and anyone may extract it. 2.4 稀少性、競爭和歧視Scarcity, competition and discrimination

A. 稀少性Scarcity

社會中每個人都面對稀少性。面對稀少資源，我們除了要作出選擇，也要與他人競爭才能使用這些資源。因此：只要稀少性存在，競爭就會出現。

Everyone in a society faces scarcity. Besides making choices, we also compete with others for scarce resources. Therefore scarcity implies competition.

B. 競爭Competition

競爭的目的是決定誰能獲得物品或資源，以及他們能獲得的數量。主要有兩種形式。

The purpose of competition is to determine who can obtain the goods or resources and how much they can get. There are two major kinds.

① 價格競爭

(1) Price competition

價格競爭以價格作為競爭準則。

Price is the criterion used in price competition.

在市場經濟中，大多數物品和資源由私人擁有且可自由轉讓，願意和有能力支付市場價格的人便可獲得它們。

In a market economy, most goods and resources are privately owned and freely transferable, so those who are willing and able to pay the market price can obtain them.

② 非價格競爭

(2) Non-price competition

非價格競爭採用價格以外的競爭準則。

Criteria other than price are used in non-price competition.

準則包括：先到先得、投票、抽籤、考試成績、外貌、性別、種族、年齡。傳統經濟可用風俗（例如較好的房間留給男性）；計劃經濟可用政治勢力。

Criteria include first-come first-served, voting, drawing lots, examination results, appearance, sex, race and age. A traditional economy may favour males by custom (for example the best rooms); a planned economy may favour those with stronger political power.

課堂例子Class examples

價格：請茶珍奶 $28，付不起的人買不到。書例：超市白米 $90，家庭主婦不願付就買不到；投資者願出 $700 萬且高於其他人，就能買到單位。非價格：校隊用體測成績選人；慈善團體只派月餅給 65 歲或以上。兩者可以同時出現：演唱會門票既要付錢，又要排隊或抽籤。

Price: a $28 Try Tea drink shuts out anyone unwilling or unable to pay. Book cases: a housewife who will not pay $90 for supermarket rice cannot buy it; an investor who offers $7 million, more than others, gets the flat. Non-price: the school team picks by a fitness test; a charity gives mooncakes only to people aged 65 or above. Both can appear together: a concert ticket may need both payment and a queue or ballot.

C. 歧視Discrimination

任何競爭都必然設有準則來區分競爭者。只有勝方才能獲得物品或資源，敗方則在競爭中受到「歧視」。因此：只要有競爭，就必然會出現歧視。

Competition must contain criteria to discriminate between competitors. Only winners get the goods or resources; losers are discriminated against. Therefore competition implies discrimination.

在多於一人的社會中，稀少性、競爭和歧視緊密相連。只要出現任何一項，即意味著另外兩者的存在。

In a society of more than one person, the existence of scarcity, competition or discrimination implies the other two.

應試攻略：一人世界沒有競爭

Exam tip: there is no competition in a one-person world

競爭是人與人之間的事

Competition is between people

書上寫「多於一人的社會」。只有一個人時，仍可有稀少性和選擇，但沒有其他人可爭，故沒有競爭，也就沒有歧視。

The book says ‘a society of more than one person’. One person can still face scarcity and must choose, but with no one else to compete against there is no competition and therefore no discrimination.

  • 正確：荒島上只有家明一個人時，他仍要選擇先採椰子還是捕魚，但沒有競爭。
  • Correct: if Ka Ming is alone on an island, he still chooses coconut or fishing, but there is no competition.
  • 易錯：把「選擇」寫成「競爭」，或說「一人世界也有歧視」。
  • Trap: calling a one-person choice ‘competition’, or saying a one-person world still has discrimination.

這裡的「歧視」是經濟學用語：按準則分出勝負，不是日常說的「不公平對待」才叫歧視。抽籤人人機會相同，落空的人仍然被歧視。書例：付不起市價 $8,888 智能手機的人，是在價格競爭中被歧視。

‘Discrimination’ here is the exam word: the criterion produces winners and losers. It is not only ‘unfair treatment’ in everyday speech. A ballot still discriminates against the unlucky. Book case: people unwilling or unable to pay the market price of a $8,599 smartphone are discriminated against by price competition.

The circular flow of economic activities shows how money, goods and services circulate among economic units within an economy.

A. 經濟活動的類型Types of economic activities

生產

Production

把投入轉化為產出的活動。投入是生產過程中使用的資源；產出是物品或服務。

Turning inputs into output. Inputs are resources used in production; outputs are goods or services.

消費

直接滿足人類慾望的活動。

The activity to satisfy human wants directly.

三種經濟活動緊密相連：有生產才有可供消費的物品和服務；消費的選擇也會影響生產。這是一個經簡化的經濟體系。

The three activities are linked: production provides goods and services for consumption; consumption choices influence production. This is a simplified economy.

  • Firms are production units in an economy.
  • 住戶是經濟體系中的消費單位。
  • Households are consumption units in an economy.

  • 生產要素市場（要素市場）：交易要素服務（或生產要素，例如勞力或土地）。
  • The factor market exchanges factor services (or factors of production such as labour and land).
  • 產品市場：交易物品和服務。
  • The product market exchanges goods and services.

(factor services; goods and services)		 住戶提供要素服務 → 廠商僱用來生產Households supply factor services → firms employ them 廠商提供物品和服務 → 住戶購買來消費Firms supply goods and services → households buy them
貨幣流程
（金錢收入和金錢支出）Money flow
(money income and money expenditure)		 廠商付生產成本 → 住戶收要素收入Firms pay production cost → households receive factor income 住戶付住戶的支出 → 廠商收廠商的收入Households pay households’ expenditure → firms receive firms’ revenue

應試攻略：對箭嘴先問「流的是東西還是錢」

Exam tip: ask first whether the arrow is stuff or money

實質流程

Real flow

住戶 → 要素市場 → 廠商：要素服務。廠商 → 產品市場 → 住戶：物品和服務。書上常用實線。

Households → factor market → firms: factor services. Firms → product market → households: goods and services. The book often uses a solid arrow.

貨幣流程

Money flow

方向相反：廠商 → 要素市場：生產成本；要素市場 → 住戶：要素收入；住戶 → 產品市場：住戶的支出；產品市場 → 廠商：廠商的收入。書上常用虛線。

The opposite way: firms → factor market: production cost; factor market → households: factor income; households → product market: households’ expenditure; product market → firms: firms’ revenue. The book often uses a dashed arrow.

  • 正確：同一筆交易，實質和貨幣方向相反。
  • Correct: for the same deal, real and money flows run opposite ways.
  • 易錯：把「廠商的收入」寫在要素市場，或把住戶當成政府。
  • Trap: putting firms’ revenue on the factor market, or labelling households as the government.

2.6 實證性陳述和規範性陳述Positive statements and normative statements

A. 實證性陳述

A. Positive statements

實證性陳述是關於事實的陳述。它們可被事實推翻。

Positive statements are statements about facts. They can be refuted by facts.

關於過去、現在和將會發生的事情。不涉及價值判斷或個人喜好。沒有事實支持或與事實不符，便會被推翻或否定。

They are about what was, what is and what will be. They do not involve value judgements or personal preferences. If unsupported or inconsistent with facts, they are refuted or rejected.

B. 規範性陳述

B. Normative statements

規範性陳述是關於價值判斷的陳述。這些陳述不能被事實推翻。

Normative statements are statements about value judgements. They cannot be refuted by facts.

關於「應該或應當是甚麼」。以個人價值判斷或喜好為主。價值判斷例如重要／不重要、好／壞；喜好例如喜歡／不喜歡。

They are about what should be or ought to be, based on personal value judgement or preferences. Value judgements: important / unimportant, good / bad. Preferences: likes / dislikes.

提提你：實證性陳述未必正確。重點是可用事實驗證，不論實際上是否成立。不要把「大多數人同意」當成實證；也不要把「與數據不符」當成規範的定義。

Watch out: a positive statement may not be true. The point is that it can be verified against facts. ‘Most people agree’ does not make a statement positive; ‘inconsistent with data’ is not the definition of normative.

題 1Q1

「請茶應該不准中四生放學後喝含糖飲料。」這是哪一類陳述？

‘Try Tea should ban sugary drinks for Form 4 students after school.’ Positive or normative?

答案：規範性陳述

Answer: a normative statement

「應該」是價值判斷，不能用數據推翻「應不應該」。

‘Should’ is a value judgement. Data cannot settle whether it ought to be done.

題 2Q2

「禁止含糖飲料後，請茶的放學生意會下跌。」這是哪一類陳述？它一定對嗎？

‘After a sugar ban, Try Tea’s after-school sales will fall.’ Positive or normative? Must it be true?

答案：實證性陳述；不一定對

Answer: positive; not necessarily true

可用銷售數據驗證。即使後來發現生意沒跌，它仍是實證性陳述。

Sales data can check it. Even if sales do not fall, the statement is still positive.

題 3Q3

「這升降機的載重極限是九個人。」這是哪一類陳述？

‘The lift’s load limit is nine people.’ Positive or normative?

答案：實證性陳述

Answer: a positive statement

可用規格或測試核對。沒有「應該」。

A specification or a test can check it. There is no ‘should’.

題 4Q4

「這升降機不應載多於九個人。」「失業比通脹更嚴重。」各是哪一類？

‘The lift should not carry more than nine people.’ ‘Unemployment is a more serious problem than inflation.’ Each is which type?

答案：兩句都是規範性陳述

Answer: both are normative

「不應」「更嚴重」都是價值判斷，不能用數據裁定應不應該、哪個較嚴重。

‘Should not’ and ‘more serious’ are value judgements. Data cannot settle what ought to be, or which problem matters more.
