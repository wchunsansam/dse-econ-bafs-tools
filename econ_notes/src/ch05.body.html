5.1A 可變生產要素和固定生產要素Variable factors and fixed factors

生產要素可按它會否隨產量的增減而變動，分為可變生產要素和固定生產要素。

Factors of production can be classified into variable factors and fixed factors by whether their quantities vary with output.

可變生產要素是指數量會隨產量的增減而變動的投入。可變成本是僱用可變生產要素的成本，它會隨產量的增減而變動。

Variable factors are inputs whose quantities vary with output. Variable costs are the costs of employing variable factors. They vary with output.

如果產量是零，廠商便毋須僱用可變生產要素，所以可變成本也是零。

If output is zero, no variable factors are employed and variable costs are zero.

固定生產要素是指數量不會隨產量的增減而變動的投入。固定成本是僱用固定生產要素的成本，它不會隨產量的增減而變動。

Fixed factors are inputs whose quantities do not vary with output. Fixed costs are the costs of employing fixed factors. They do not vary with output.

即使產量是零，廠商仍須僱用固定生產要素（除非廠商結業），所以固定成本是正數。

Even if output is zero, fixed factors may still be employed (unless the business closes down), and fixed costs are positive.

5.1B 短期和長期Short run and long run

按生產是否涉及固定生產要素，生產時期可分為短期和長期。

There are two production runs: the short run and the long run. They are classified by whether fixed factors exist.

在短期，至少有一個生產要素是固定生產要素，其餘是可變生產要素。

Short run is the period over which at least one factor of production is a fixed factor and other factors are variable factors.

在長期，所有生產要素都是可變生產要素，沒有固定生產要素。

Long run is the period over which all factors of production are variable factors. There are no fixed factors.

固定 + 可變Fixed + variable → 短期Short run
只有可變Variable factors only → 長期Long run

應試攻略 5.1：生產時期不是按時間長短來劃分

Exam Tips 5.1: Production runs are not classified by the length of the time period

看有沒有固定生產要素

Look for a fixed factor

不論時間多長，只要至少有一個固定生產要素，生產就是短期。

No matter how long the time period is, production is in the short run if there is at least one fixed factor.

不同行業調校全部要素所需的時間不同

Industries need different lengths of time to adjust every factor

便利店開新分店和聘新人，可能要兩個月。電力公司興建新電廠和擴展電網，可能要數年。兩段都是該行業的長期，因為所有生產要素都可以變動。

A convenience store may take two months to open a new branch and hire new staff. An electricity company may take several years to build a new power plant and extend the network. Both periods are the long run for that industry, because every factor can be varied.

5.2A 產量的量度Measurement of output

在短期，由於固定生產要素的數量不會隨產量增減而變動，因此我們要量度不同數量的可變生產要素的產量。

In the short run, as the amount of fixed factors does not change with output, we measure output under different quantities/amounts of variable factors.

總產量（TP）是在指定期間內，某數量的可變生產要素所生產的產出數量（即產量）。

Total product (TP) is the amount of output produced by a given quantity of a variable factor during a specified period.

「產量」和「產出」都可以指總產量。

The terms ‘output’ and ‘total output’ (Q) can also refer to total product.

平均產量（AP）是在指定的期間內，平均每單位可變生產要素的產量。

Average product (AP) is the amount of output produced by a unit of a variable factor on average during a specified period, i.e., the output per unit of a variable factor employed.

邊際產量（MP）是在指定的期間內，額外僱用一單位可變生產要素所引致的總產量變動。

Marginal product (MP) is the change in total product from employing an extra unit of a variable factor during a specified period.

MPn = TPn − TPn−1

MPn：第 n 個單位可變生產要素的邊際產量。TPn：n 單位可變生產要素的總產量。TPn−1：(n − 1) 單位可變生產要素的總產量。

MPn: MP of the nth unit of a variable factor. TPn: TP of n units of a variable factor. TPn−1: TP of (n − 1) units of a variable factor.

5.2B 邊際回報遞減定律Law of diminishing marginal returns

在短期，廠商不能透過不斷增加可變生產要素來無限制地增加總產量。我們可利用邊際回報遞減定律來解釋這個情況。

In the short run, a firm cannot increase its total product infinitely by increasing variable factors. This is illustrated by the law of diminishing marginal returns.

邊際回報遞減定律指出，假設科技水平不變，在短期，當更多的可變生產要素不斷地加到某定額的固定生產要素時，該可變生產要素的邊際產量最終會下降。這個定律又稱為邊際產量遞減定律。

The law of diminishing marginal returns states that in the short run, when more units of a variable factor are continuously added to a given quantity of fixed factors, the marginal product of the variable factor will eventually decrease, holding technology constant. The law is also called the law of diminishing marginal product.

① 為甚麼邊際產量最初可能會增加？

① Why may marginal product initially increase?

這可能是因為固定生產要素（機器）最初未被充分利用。增加更多可變生產要素（勞力）可以更有效運用固定生產要素。

This may be because the fixed factor (machinery) is not fully utilised initially. Adding more units of the variable factor (labour) can help utilise the fixed factor more efficiently.

② 為甚麼邊際產量最終必然下降？

② Why must marginal product eventually decrease?

當僱用更多可變生產要素（勞力）時，所有固定生產要素（機器）會得到充分的運用。隨後額外增加的可變生產要素就沒有足夠的固定生產要素可供運用。

When more units of the variable factor (labour) are employed, all fixed factors (machinery) are fully used by the variable factor. After that, extra units of the variable factor do not have sufficient fixed factors to work with.

固定生產要素的產能有限，就是邊際回報遞減定律只適用於短期的原因。

Such limited capacity of fixed factors explains why the law of diminishing marginal returns only applies in the short run.

農夫的邊際產量遞減（課本 p.11）

Diminishing marginal products of farmers (Textbook p. 11)

當農夫在一塊農地（固定生產要素）上增加工作時數（可變生產要素）時，農地的固定面積限制了農夫額外勞力的邊際產量。否則，只需一塊農地就能夠生產足夠食物養活全世界。

When farmers work longer hours (variable factor) on a piece of farmland (fixed factor), the size of the farmland limits the marginal product of additional efforts of farmers. Otherwise, a piece of farmland can produce sufficient food to feed the entire world.

即使我們工作更長時數或僱用更多農夫，這塊農田的產量也無法再增加。

The yield of this parcel of farmland cannot be increased even if we work longer hours or hire more farmers.

Marginal cost (MC) is the change in total cost of producing an extra unit of output during a specified period. MCx = TCx − TCx−1. The calculation of MC will be discussed in detail in Chapter 9.

在長期，由於沒有固定生產要素，TFC = 0。

In the long run, as fixed cost does not exist, TFC = 0.

5.4A 擴大生產規模的結果Results of increasing the scale of production

在長期，所有生產要素都是可變的。當廠商增加所有生產要素的數量時，生產規模便會擴大。

In the long run, all factors of production are variable. When a firm increases its employment of all factors of production to increase output, its scale of production increases.

當廠商擴大生產規模時，總成本會上升，但長期平均成本則可能會隨着產量增加而上升或下降，這取決於以下兩項經濟原則：

When a firm expands its scale of production, its total cost will increase. However, the long run average cost (LRAC) may increase or decrease with output based on the following economic principles:

長期平均成本（LRAC）是指在長期，平均生產一單位產出的成本。LRAC = 總成本（TC）/ 產量（Q）。

Long run average cost (LRAC) is the cost of producing a unit of output on average in the long run. LRAC = TC / Q.

規模經濟是指擴大生產規模的好處，會令長期平均生產成本下降。

Economies of scale refer to the advantages associated with a larger scale of production that lower the long run average cost of production.

規模 ↑ → 產量 ↑ → LRAC ↓

Scale ↑ → output ↑ → LRAC ↓

規模負經濟是指擴大生產規模的壞處，會令長期平均生產成本上升。

Diseconomies of scale refer to the disadvantages associated with a larger scale of production that raise the long run average cost of production.

規模 ↑ → 產量 ↑ → LRAC ↑

Scale ↑ → output ↑ → LRAC ↑

當廠商不斷擴大生產規模以增加產量時，通常最初會出現規模經濟，最終會出現規模負經濟。因此，廠商的長期平均成本曲線呈 U 形。最優規模是 LRAC 的最低點。

When a firm continuously expands its scale of production to raise output, it normally faces economies of scale initially and diseconomies of scale eventually. Hence, a firm’s LRAC curve is U-shaped. The optimal scale is LRAC at a minimum.

規模經濟，LRAC ↓Economies of scale, LRAC ↓ → 最優規模，LRAC 最低Optimal scale, LRAC at a minimum → 規模負經濟，LRAC ↑Diseconomies of scale, LRAC ↑

生產規模超過最優規模，廠商不一定虧損。最優規模是平均成本最低，不是利润必定為正或必定為負的那一點。

A firm producing beyond the optimal scale is not necessarily making a loss. The optimal scale is the lowest average cost. It is not the point where profit must be positive or must be negative.

規模經濟和規模負經濟屬於長期概念，必須在廠商沒有固定生產要素／沒有固定成本的情況下，這兩個概念才適用。

Economies of scale and diseconomies of scale are long run concepts. To apply these concepts, the firm must have no fixed factors / no fixed costs.

(d) After all inputs increase, the percentage rise in output is higher than the percentage rise in average cost.

答案：規模負經濟。平均成本仍然上升。

Answer: diseconomies of scale. Average cost still rises.

5.4B 規模經濟和規模負經濟的成因Sources of economies and diseconomies of scale

規模經濟和規模負經濟可分為兩類：內部（廠商自身）和外部（整個行業）。課程不要求背成因的名稱，要能按情境解釋長期平均成本為何下降或上升。

Economies of scale and diseconomies of scale can be divided into two categories: internal (within a firm) and external (within an industry). The names of the sources are not required. What is required is an explanation, in the given situation, of why long run average cost falls or rises.

內部Internal

內部規模經濟是指廠商擴大自身的生產規模時可享有的好處，令長期平均生產成本得以下降。

Internal economies of scale refer to the advantages that a firm enjoys when it enlarges its own scale of production, resulting in a lower long run average cost of production.

  • 員工之間可以有更精細的分工，管理效率和生產力上升。
  • A more extensive division of labour raises managerial efficiency and productivity.
  • 較大的廠商可提供較多抵押品，能以較低利率向銀行借款。
  • A larger firm can offer more collateral and may borrow at a lower interest rate.
  • 大量購買原材料，可以獲得較大折扣。
  • Buying raw materials in bulk can bring a larger discount.
  • 市場推廣成本，以及研究和發展成本，可以攤分到較大產量。
  • Marketing costs, and research and development costs, can be spread over a larger output.
  • 機器和設備可以更充分運用，生產力上升。
  • Machines and equipment can be used more fully, so productivity rises.

內部規模負經濟是指廠商過度擴大自身的生產規模而要面對的壞處，長期平均生產成本會上升。

Internal diseconomies of scale refer to the disadvantages that a firm suffers when it enlarges its own scale of production beyond the optimal scale, resulting in a higher long run average cost of production.

  • 架構過於複雜，協調變弱，決策變慢，管理效率下降。
  • The organisation becomes too complicated, coordination weakens, decisions slow down, and managerial efficiency falls.
  • 若已有大量未償還貸款，再借款時可能要付較高利率。
  • If the firm already has large outstanding loans, further loans may require a higher interest rate.
  • 所需投入供應有限時，可能要以較高價格購買，或採用較昂貴的替代品。
  • When the supply of necessary inputs is limited, the firm may buy more expensive inputs or use more expensive substitutes.
  • 市場飽和時，推廣產品的市場推廣成本上升。
  • When the market is saturated, marketing costs of promoting the product rise.

生活實例 5.1　WeWork 過度擴張Living Case 5.1　Overexpansion of WeWork

課本 p.25Textbook p. 25

WeWork 把辦公室分成較小的共用空間再租出。2019 年它在 110 個城市經營，是美國最大的初創公司之一。2023 年因過度擴張和管理不善申請破產。

WeWork divides office premises into smaller co-working spaces and lets them out. In 2019 it operated in 110 cities and was one of the biggest startups in the US. In 2023 it filed for bankruptcy after overexpansion and poor management.

擴張初期可能享有的內部規模經濟：大量購買辦公用品和傢俬可獲折扣，亦可與業主議得較低租金；市場推廣成本，以及預約系統和會員系統的開發成本，可以攤分到更多共用空間。

Internal economies it may have enjoyed at first: discounts on office supplies and furniture bought in bulk, and lower rents negotiated with landlords; marketing costs, and the cost of developing the booking and membership systems, spread over more co-working spaces.

過度擴張後可能面對的內部規模負經濟：累積 29 億美元債務，再借款可能要付較高利率；共用空間市場可能飽和，推廣成本上升。

Internal diseconomies it may have faced after overexpanding: US$2.9 billion of debt, so further loans may cost a higher interest rate; the co-working market may be saturated, so marketing costs rise.

外部External

外部規模經濟是指個別廠商所屬的行業擴張時，它可享有的好處，令長期平均生產成本得以下降。

External economies of scale refer to the advantages that an individual firm enjoys when the industry expands, resulting in a lower long run average cost of production.

  • 更多工人受相關訓練，招聘有經驗工人的成本下降。
  • More workers are trained for the industry, so the cost of recruiting experienced workers falls.
  • 行業更知名，吸引更多顧客，個別廠商的市場推廣成本下降。
  • The industry becomes better known and attracts more buyers, so an individual firm’s marketing costs fall.
  • 維修、運輸、貯存等支援服務更多，採購成本下降。
  • More back-up services, such as repair, transport and storage, lower the cost of sourcing.
  • 行業集中在一個地區時，交通網絡可能改善，運輸成本下降。
  • If the industry clusters in one area, the transport network may improve and transport costs fall.

外部規模負經濟是指個別廠商所屬的行業過度擴張時，它要面對的壞處，令長期平均生產成本上升。

External diseconomies of scale refer to the disadvantages that an individual firm suffers when the industry expands beyond the optimal scale, resulting in a higher long run average cost of production.

  • 勞力、廠房和原材料的需求大增，工資、租金和原料價格上升。
  • Demand for labour, factories and raw materials rises sharply, so wages, rent and raw-material prices rise.
  • 市場飽和，個別廠商的市場推廣成本上升。
  • The market may be saturated, so an individual firm’s marketing costs rise.
  • 支援服務的產能有限，服務價格上升，採購成本上升。
  • Back-up services have limited capacity, their prices rise, and the cost of sourcing rises.

生活實例 5.2　同類廠商傾向聚在一區Living Case 5.2　Similar firms tend to cluster

課本 p.28Textbook p. 28

同類廠商聚在一起，可能享有外部規模經濟：市場推廣成本較低、運輸成本較低、支援服務較多、較易招聘有經驗的工人。香港科學園有超過 1,400 間資訊科技公司。油麻地果欄有超過 200 個水果檔。不少電器店集中在同一個商場。

When similar firms cluster, they may enjoy external economies of scale: lower marketing costs, lower transport costs, more back-up services, and easier recruitment of experienced workers. Hong Kong Science Park has over 1,400 IT companies. Yau Ma Tei Wholesale Fruit Market has over 200 fruit stalls. Many electronics shops sit in the same shopping mall.

例題拆解 5.3　內部和外部規模經濟Guided Example 5.3　Internal and external economies of scale

課本 p.29Textbook p. 29

某教育機構為文憑試考生舉辦模擬考試。解釋下列情況下，該機構的長期平均成本為何改變。每項寫兩個原因。

An educational organisation holds mock exams for HKDSE candidates. Explain, with two reasons, why its long run average cost changes in each case.

（a）同一科目的模擬考試場次增加後，長期平均成本下降。

(a) After it holds more sessions of the mock exam for the same subject, long run average cost falls.

（b）更多機構都舉辦文憑試模擬考試後，這間機構發現長期平均成本上升。

(b) After more organisations hold HKDSE mock exams, this organisation finds that long run average cost rises.

解題思路

Thinking process

先看平均成本上升還是下降。再看是自己的規模擴大，還是整個行業擴大。解釋要扣著情境，不要只列名稱。

First see whether average cost rises or falls. Then see whether the firm’s own scale expands, or the industry expands. The explanation has to use the situation. Do not only list names.

（a）答案：內部規模經濟。廣告成本可以攤分到更多考試場次，平均市場推廣成本下降。員工可以有更精細的分工，管理效率上升。
