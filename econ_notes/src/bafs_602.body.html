1　簡介Introduction

市場研究是運用客觀和系統化的方法收集資料（收集、記錄及分析），以幫助有關人員作出市場營銷決策的過程。

Marketing research is a systematic process of gathering, recording and analyzing of information about problems relating to the sales of goods/services to customers that can aid in making marketing decisions.

中文標題「學習重點」印成「學習重黠」，這裡用「重點」。兩種語言的定義句都沒有畫紅，所以定義本身不是填空。

The Chinese heading prints 學習重黠; this note uses 重點. Neither language marks the definition sentence, so the definition is not a blank.

2　市場研究的重要性Importance of marketing research

識別和評估市場上各種機會和危機Identify and evaluate market opportunities and threats

市場研究能為企業提供有關目前經營環境的資料。企業因此可以進行強弱機危分析，以了解所面對的機會和危機，並採取相應行動。

Marketing research can provide information about a company’s operating environment. This allows the company to do a SWOT analysis.

分析細分市場和選擇目標市場Analyse market segments and select target markets

市務人員可透過市場研究知道每個細分市場的特性。從而選擇最適合的細分市場作為目標市場。

Marketing research can provide useful information about the characteristics of each segments. This helps marketers choose the most suitable segment.

制定市場營銷策略以滿足顧客需要Formulate and implement marketing strategies to satisfy consumer needs

市場研究提供有關顧客需求及需要的資訊。因此這幫助市務人員設計合適的市場營銷策略。

Marketing research can provide information about customer needs or wants. This helps marketers design appropriate marketing strategies.

評估市場營銷表現Evaluate market performance

市場研究提供企業在市場營銷方面實際表現的資訊。因此這有助市場營銷經理更有效地監控市場營銷活動。

Marketing research can provide information about a company’s actual marketing performance. This helps marketing managers control marketing activities more effectively.

中文「制定策略」那格，紅字只在「合適」。顧客需求及需要沒有畫紅。英文畫的是 customer needs、wants 和 appropriate。英文 “each segments” 按影印本保留，不改成 each segment。

In the Chinese strategy card, only 合適 is red. 顧客需求及需要 is not. English marks customer needs, wants and appropriate. The photocopy’s “each segments” is kept.

4　市場研究的主要目標The major objectives of marketing research

一般來說，企業進行市場研究有三大目標。

Three main objectives for the company to conduct marketing research.

制定恰當的市場營銷計劃To do proper marketing planning

  • 現時顧客的特性
  • The characteristics of people who buy its products
  • 現時市場的前景
  • The prospects of its existing markets
  • 其他新市場的潛力
  • The potential of other new markets

調整現有的市場營銷策略To fine-tune existing marketing strategies

消費者對＿＿＿＿的反應

Consumers’ responses to……

  • 企業產品 (例如包裝設計)
  • its products (e.g. product design, packaging design)
  • 產品目前價格水平 (例如價格上升或折扣)
  • its pricing strategies (e.g. existing price level, price increase, discount)
  • 銷售產品的地方 (例如超級市場、便利店或互聯網)
  • its place strategies (e.g. where to buy, supermarket, convenience store, internet)
  • 企業各種推廣組合工具(例如廣告或個人銷售)
  • its promotion mix tools (e.g. advertising, personal selling)

有效地監控市場營銷活動To properly control marketing activities

  • 消費者對其產品的滿意程度
  • Consumers’ degree of satisfaction with its products
  • 產品的整體表現 (例如市場佔有率)
  • Performance (e.g. market share) of its products
  • 產品在大眾心目中的地位
  • Its reputation among the general public

中文價格那一行寫「產品目前價格水平」。英文寫 pricing strategies，例子裡有 existing price level、price increase、discount。中文推廣例子是廣告或個人銷售；英文在 advertising 和 personal selling 之間同樣畫成一整段。

Chinese names the price line 產品目前價格水平. English names it pricing strategies, with existing price level, price increase and discount inside the same red span. Both languages mark the promotion line as one span.

5　收集數據的方法（原始數據 vs 二手數據）Data collection methods (primary data vs secondary data)

四種方法的標題沒有畫紅。紅字在每種方法裡面的詞。

The four method titles are not red. The blanks are the words inside each method.

5.1　調查研究法Survey research method

調查研究法（又稱問卷調查）是透過問卷有系統地收集所需資料的方法。最常用於收集原始數據。通常用來收集描述性資料，例如研究顧客的態度（對產品的滿足感及偏好）及意識（對產品的認識）。

Survey research method (also called Quantitative research) is a systematic method for collecting needed information through questionnaire. It is used for collecting descriptive information, for example consumers’ attitudes (satisfaction and preferences towards their products) and awareness (knowledge about their products).

中文括號裡的紅字是「問卷調查」。英文括號裡的紅間線是 Quantitative research。不要把英文改成 questionnaire survey，也不要把中文改成量化研究。

The Chinese bracket marks 問卷調查. The English bracket marks Quantitative research. Do not swap those two names.

例子：巴士公司設計問卷，調查司機的態度、巴士的準時程度及駕駛的安全程度。

Example: a bus company designs a questionnaire about drivers’ attitude, punctuality and safety of driving.

問卷包含一系列問題。問卷調查通常用來收集大量研究對象（通常多於 100 人）的意見。意見通常會以統計學的方法進行分析。（例如計算平均值及繪畫圖表） 紅字備註：More representative, concrete info。

A questionnaire contains a set of specifically designed questions. It obtains responses from a relatively large number of people (>100) called respondents. Responses are usually analysed by statistical methods. [use of charts, means] Red note: More representative, concrete info.

調查聯絡方式（課本 P. 45）：(1) 個人面談 (2) 電話訪問 (3) 郵件 (4) 電子媒介。這四項沒有畫紅。

Survey contact methods (textbook P. 45): (1) personal interviews (2) telephone interviews (3) mail (4) electronic means. Those four are not marked.

5.2　質性研究法Qualitative research method

質性研究法主要透過少數的受訪者（通常少於３０人）獲得較具啟發性的資料，從而對研究議題有更深入的認識。紅字備註：[之前沒有任何概念] 新的產品。用於探究性研究，了解顧客消費行為的動機及心理因素。

Qualitative research gains more insight from a relatively small number of people (<30). The “under 30” phrase is not marked in English. Red note: [do not have any idea before] new products (given sample). It is used in exploratory research, to understand customers’ motives and psychological thinking.

面談訪問In-depth interview

以個人面談的形式進行，受訪者一般熟悉議題。多利用開放式問題，受訪者就能自由發表意見。英文課本 P. 48 圖 2.8。

A personal interview with an interviewee who knows the research issue well. It is usually unstructured so the interviewee can express views freely. Open-ended question. Textbook P. 48 Fig 2.8.

聚焦小組Focus group discussion

小組通常有 6 至 10 名經過精心挑選的參與者。主持人負責提問參與者，並鼓勵他們參與討論，以提供與研究議題有關的資料。

A group of carefully selected respondents (6–10). The moderator’s role is to provide discussion questions, encourage respondents to participate, and keep the discussion on the track.

中文面談訪問畫的是個人面談、熟悉議題、開放式、自由。英文標題畫的是 In-depth，句中畫的是 freely，Open-ended question 是紅字。中文「少於 30 人」是紅字；英文 <30 沒有畫線。

Chinese interview blanks are 個人面談, 熟悉議題, 開放式 and 自由. English marks In-depth, freely, and the red words Open-ended question. Chinese marks 少於３０人. English does not mark <30.

5.3　觀察法Observational research method

觀察法是指直接記錄事實訊息，或在自然或預設場景中觀察和記錄人們的行為表現。紅字：更客觀及準確、避免尷尬。為了避免受訪者隱瞞真實的想法。（小朋友）

Observational research is the process of observing and recording people’s actual behavior. Red notes: more objective & accurate, Avoid embarrassment. It is used when people are unwilling or unable to provide information (e.g. children).

中文畫的是「隱瞞」。英文畫的是 unwilling 和 unable，不是 conceal 的直譯。

Chinese marks 隱瞞. English marks unwilling and unable, not a translation of 隱瞞.

自然場景：神秘顧客觀察顧客是否滿意。預設場景：玩具房觀察小朋友怎樣選玩具，藉此觀察他們的喜好。影印本寫「籍此觀念」，這裡改成「藉此觀察」。

Natural setting: secret shoppers. Planned setting: a toy room. Textbook P. 51. The Chinese note prints 籍此觀念; this note uses 藉此觀察.

5.4　實驗研究法Experimental research method

實驗研究法是一種量化研究法，目的是解釋變數之間的因果關係。用於因果性研究，須控制無關的因素，並檢查這些羣組之間的不同反應及因果關係。研究人員隨機把顧客分組。不同組別行為的差異會被量度。

Experimental research aims at explaining the cause-and-effect relationship between variables. That opening “cause-and-effect” is not marked. Causal research tries to explain cause and effect relationships between experimental variables (aspects of marketing mix, e.g. launch TV commercial) and dependent variables (usually sales or profit). Researchers randomly divide consumers into groups. The variation of behaviour is measured.

  • 零售商比較兩間分店的銷售額，一間有優惠活動，另一間沒有。
  • Retailers try promotional discounts in one outlet and compare sales with outlets that do not offer them. Textbook P. 47 Fig 2.7.
  • 口味測試：不同口味的朱古力，看哪個最受歡迎。
  • Taste tests: chocolate in different flavours. The photocopy spells “favours”.

中文「因果關係」出現兩次，兩次都是紅字。英文開頭的 cause-and-effect 沒有畫線，後面解釋句的 cause and effect 才是紅間線。

Chinese marks 因果關係 twice. English does not mark the opening cause-and-effect. It marks cause and effect in the causal-research sentence.

請茶兩檔試少糖Try Tea tests less sugar at two stalls

小明在學校門口的檔貼「少糖」牌，屋邨的檔不貼。兩檔茶底一樣。一周後比杯數。這是看推廣和銷量，不是只問人喜不喜歡。

Bob puts a “less sugar” sign at the school-gate stall and not at the estate stall. The tea base is the same. After a week he compares cups sold. That looks at promotion and sales, not only at whether people say they like it.

6　抽樣Sampling

例子：一間化妝品公司想研究香港女士喜不喜歡新的唇膏。

Example: a cosmetic company wants to find out if Hong Kong women like its new lipsticks.

術語Term 意思Meaning 圖上的紅字例子Red labels on the diagram
目標總體Target population 市場研究人員有興趣進行研究的所有研究對象the population which a researcher is interested in studying All women in Hong Kong
樣本Sample 目標總體的一部分a subset of a target population 300 selected
樣本元素Sample elements 獲選為樣本的研究對象respondents being included in a sample for study HK women

要抽取有代表性的樣本，必須考慮三項主要因素。

To obtain a representative sample, consider three factors.

  • 抽樣單位：誰是調查對象？(= 目標市場)
  • Sampling unit: Who is to be surveyed? (= target market)
  • 樣本容量：樣本應包含多少名調查對象
  • Sample size: How many people should be surveyed?
  • 抽樣方法：如何挑選受訪者？
  • Sampling procedure: How should respondents be chosen? sampling techniques

中文三項因素裡，紅字只在「代表性」和「(= 目標市場)」。英文還畫了 Who、How many、How，以及 sampling techniques。

Chinese marks 代表性 and (= 目標市場) among the three factors. English also marks Who, How many, How, and sampling techniques.

隨機抽樣Simple random sampling 分層隨機抽樣Stratified random sampling 便利抽樣Convenience sampling
意思Meaning 每個元素被抽中的機會是可知及均等的Every member has equal chance of being selected 按年齡、收入或教育程度等分為多個小組（稱為層），然後從每個小組中隨機挑選樣本元素The population is divided into mutually exclusive groups and random samples are drawn from each group 挑選最容易接觸到的元素Subjects are selected because of their convenient accessibility
例子Examples

從擁有全部學生名字的盒子中抽獎。健身中心從會員名單選 20 位會員。

Drawing names from a box with all respondents’ names. A fitness centre chooses 20 members from its membership list.

收入水平（低、中、高）對購買行為。年齡組別對政治價值。反映了總體的多樣性。

Income level against purchasing behaviour. Age groups against political views. Reflect the diversity of the population.

訪問街上的行人或商場的消費者。電台呼籲觀眾打去表達意見。回應不能代表目標總體的意願。

Interviewing passers-by or shoppers in a mall. A radio station asks citizens to call in. Convenient samples may not represent the target population.
抽樣架構Sampling frame 需要needed 需要needed 不需要NO need

抽樣架構是一份列出目標總體中所有元素的名單。「需要／不需要」在影印本沒有畫紅，所以留在表上。

A sampling frame is a list that identifies every element in the target population. Needed / NO need is not marked, so those words stay visible.

中文隨機抽樣畫「可知」和「均等」。英文只畫 equal。中文分層畫「多個小組」「小組」「挑選」。英文畫 mutually exclusive groups。中文便利抽樣畫「最容易接觸到」和「代表」。英文畫 convenient accessibility 和 represent。

Chinese random sampling marks 可知 and 均等. English marks only equal. Chinese stratified sampling marks 多個小組, 小組 and 挑選. English marks mutually exclusive groups. Chinese convenience sampling marks 最容易接觸到 and 代表. English marks convenient accessibility and represent.

請茶問誰Who Try Tea asks

小新站在門口問剛買完的人。那是最容易接觸到的人，不能代表沒來過的學生。小麗若果從會員名單每班抽同樣人數，才是分層之後再隨機挑。

Sam asks people who have just bought a cup. Those are the people easiest to reach, and they do not stand for students who never come. If Eslyn draws the same number from each class on the member list, that is a random draw after the list is split into groups.
