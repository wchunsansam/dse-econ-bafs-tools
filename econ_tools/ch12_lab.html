512 貿易障礙互動教具512 Trade barriers lab

考試會反覆打的機制：小型經濟是受價者，Pw 不因本地進口量而變；關稅把本地價推高 T，政府拿走長方形；配額畫折曲供應，面積 B 不一定歸政府。Exam mechanisms: a small economy is a price taker so Pw does not move with its imports; a tariff raises the domestic price by T and the government takes the rectangle; a quota kinks supply and area B need not go to the government.
