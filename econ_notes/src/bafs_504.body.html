1　資本投資Capital investment

資本投資是與企業生產或營運相關的長期投資。它通常涉及固定資產的投資。主要目的是提高企業的盈利能力。

Capital investment refers to long-term investment that is related to production or the operation of a business. It is investment in fixed assets. Its major purpose is to increase the firm’s profitability.

這一章的資本投資是長期，不是短期。不要把買原材料或捱過週末的透支寫進資本投資。

Capital investment in this chapter is long-term, not short-term. Do not treat a materials purchase or a weekend overdraft as a capital investment.

2　五個財務因素Five financial factors

以下每一句都假設其他因素不變。因素上升時，吸引力只跟該行的方向走。

Every sentence below holds other factors constant. When that factor rises, attractiveness moves only in the direction of that row.

2.1　投資成本Investment costs

投資成本越高，項目的吸引力便越低。

The higher the investment costs, the less attractive the investment, holding other factors constant.

投資成本有兩種：初期投資成本，以及其後的成本。

Two types of investment cost: initial investment outlay, and subsequent costs.

例子Example 初期Initial 其後Subsequent
新機器New machine 購買價＋安裝費Purchase price + installation cost 維修費Maintenance cost
租購的影印機Photocopying machine by hire purchase 首付The down payment 安裝費，連利息Instalments, including the interest

2.2　投資收入Investment income

投資收入（未來現金流量＋預期的殘值）越高，項目的吸引力便越大。

Investment income (the future stream of cash flows + the expected salvage value) — the higher it is, the more attractive the investment, holding other factors constant.

現金流入有兩種：直接收入，以及所節省的成本。買機器或設備時，現金流入往往是省下來的成本，不一定是新的銷售額。

Cash inflows: (1) direct income, (2) cost reduction / cost savings. For the purchase of machines or equipment, the cash inflow is often the cost saved, not a new stream of sales.

2.3　投資期Investment horizon

投資期（項目預期的年份）愈長，項目的吸引力便越小。

The investment horizon is the expected years of the project. The longer it is, the less attractive the investment, holding other factors constant. The project is less liquid.

原因：這些項目可能會長期凍結資金，迫使企業放棄其他投資機會。

Such projects may lock up the firm’s capital for a long period of time and force the firm to give up other investment opportunities.

2.4　金錢的時間值（資本成本）Time value of money (cost of capital)

今天收到的一元比將來收到的一元更有價值，因為今天的金錢可以用作投資來賺取利息。

A dollar on hand today is worth more than a dollar to be received in the future, because the dollar on hand today can be invested to earn interest and yield more than a dollar in the future.

公式：PV = FV / (1+i)n。i 可以叫貼現率、資本成本或所要求的回報率。

Formula: PV = FV / (1+i)n, where i = discount rate / cost of capital / required rate of return.

資本成本是指第二個最佳選擇賺取的回報率。這反映了企業投資固定資產的成本。

Cost of capital is the rate of return that one earns from the next best alternative. It reflects the firm’s cost of obtaining capital to invest in long-term assets.

貼現率越高，現金流入的現值便越低，從投資項目獲得的回報也越低。

The higher the discount rate, the lower the present value of the cash inflows, and the lower the return from the investment project.

2.5　資本投資項目的風險Riskiness of capital projects

未來現金流量，特別是現金流入，越是難以預料，項目的投資風險便越高。

An investment is riskier if there is more uncertainty about future cash flows.

風險和回報一般呈正向關係。項目風險較高，企業會要求較高的回報率。

Risk and return are positively related. A firm will demand a higher rate of return from a riskier project.

如果預期的回報率低過所要求的回報率，企業不會投資該項目。

If the expected rate of return is lower than the required rate of return, the firm will not engage in the project.

Correct: more uncertain cash flows mean a riskier project and a higher required return; if the expected return is lower than the required return, do not invest. Trap: writing that risk and return are negatively related.

3　五個非財務因素Five non-financial factors

非財務因素是跟金錢沒有直接關係的因素。項目即使無利可圖，企業仍可能因為下面的原因而接受。

A non-financial factor is any factor not related to money. The firm may accept a project even though it is not profitable.

3.1　策略性原因Strategic reasons

如果資本項目有助企業達成策略性目標，即使該項目無利可圖，企業也可能接受。

If a capital project helps the firm achieve its strategic goals, the firm may accept the project even though it is not profitable.

  • 目標：成為市場領導者（擴大市場佔有率）。決策：建立新產品、擴大零售網絡。
  • Goal: to be the market leader (acquire more market share). Projects: develop new products, expand the retail network.
  • 目標：發掘新的技術。決策：收購另一間公司。
  • Goal: to possess new technology. Project: acquire another firm.

3.2　遵守監管法規Regulatory compliance

政府可能要求企業安裝特定設施，以盡量減少污染和降低安全風險。

The government may require firms to install certain facilities and equipment to minimise pollution and safety risks.

例子：節能電器。

Examples: electricity generation and construction.

3.3　符合行業標準Matching industry standards

行業訂立更嚴格的標準時，企業便可能要添置新機器和設備，否則便會失去競爭力。

The firm may have to invest in new machines and equipment in order to comply with the new standard, otherwise it loses competitiveness in the industry.

例子：國際標準化組織的品質管理標準（ISO 9000）。

Example: international standards of quality management (ISO 9000).

筆記個案　Toyota 2009–2010Notes case　Toyota 2009–2010

Toyota 在 2009–2010 年全球回收約 800 萬輛有油門踏板問題的車輛。筆記用這條鏈說明品質標準一旦失手：嚴格成本控制 → 品質下降；擴大生產 → 較少著重品質控制 → 優質可靠的名聲受損 → 顧客失去信心。

Toyota recalled about 8 million vehicles globally in 2009–2010 because of accelerator-pedal problems. The notes use this chain: strict cost controls → quality lowered; expand production → less emphasis on quality control → the reputation for superior quality and reliability is damaged → customers lose confidence.

3.4　提升企業形象Improving business image

企業為了提升形象，可能會接受無利可圖或利潤較低的資本項目。

A firm may accept an unprofitable or low-profit capital project in order to improve its image.

  • 雖然租金貴、利潤減少，仍可能在熱門地區開設分店。
  • Open a branch on Canton Road although the rent is expensive.
  • 到發展程度較低的地區擴充業務。
  • Expand the business into less-developed countries.
  • 投資環保項目（使用節能電器），建立環保、負責任的良好市民形象。
  • Invest in environmentally friendly projects (energy-saving machines, or new waste-treatment methods) and build an image of a responsible corporate citizen.

3.5　提高員工士氣Improving staff morale

企業可以透過改善工作環境來提高員工士氣，因此接受一些資本項目。

A firm may accept capital projects to improve its workplace environment and thus staff morale.

例子：更先進的設備；更寬敞、通風的工作環境。

Examples: more advanced equipment; a more spacious workplace; better ventilation.

後廚抽氣The kitchen exhaust

小麗說屯門請茶的後廚又熱又窄，兼職做到兩小時就想走。加裝抽氣和加寬工作台未必立刻增加利潤，但工作環境改善，員工士氣會高一點。這是非財務因素，不是回收期算得出來的銷售額。

A better exhaust and a wider bench may not raise profit at once, but the workplace is better and morale is higher. That is a non-financial factor, not a sales figure the payback period can see.

非財務因素也可以從其他主要企業功能進入。下面都不是金錢本身。

Non-financial factors can also enter through the other key business functions. None of these is money itself.

4A　回收期Payback period

回收期是指企業收回投資成本所需的時間，又稱為歸本期或歸本年期。單位是年。

The payback period is the length of time required for the stream of cash flows from an investment to recover the original cash outlay. The unit is years.

計算Calculation

第 1 步：每年尚未收回的成本Step 1: remaining cost yet to be recovered each year → 第 2 步：數要多少年才收回Step 2: count the years needed to recover the cost → 不足一年就用分數年Use a fraction if recovery falls inside a year

回收期 = 有剩餘成本的上年年份 + 去年的剩餘成本 ÷ 今年的現金流量

Payback period = last year with remaining cost yet to be recovered + remaining cost from last year ÷ cash flow from this year

筆記練習。第 0 年現金流出 $1,000。其後每年的現金流入如下。第 3 年某時收支平衡。

Notes exercise. Cash outflow at year 0 is $1,000. Later cash inflows are below. Breakeven falls sometime in year 3.

年Year 現金流量 ($)Cash flow ($) 剩餘成本 ($)Remaining cost ($)
0 −1,000 1,000
1 500 500
2 400 100
3 200 0
4 200
5 100

回收期 = 2 + 100 ÷ 200 = 2.5 年。第 4 年及第 5 年的現金流不改變這個年數。

Payback period = 2 + 100 ÷ 200 = 2.5 years. The cash flows in year 4 and year 5 do not change that figure.

決策規則Decision rule

可同時進行的項目

Projects that can be taken together

只要回收期短於或等於可接受的最長回收期，便接受該項目。

Accept as long as the payback period is less than or equal to the maximum the management allows.

互相排斥的項目

Mutually exclusive projects

在已符合最長回收期的項目之間，選擇回收期較短的一個。

Among projects whose payback period is within the maximum allowed, accept the one with the shortest payback period.

回收期愈長，風險愈高。不要寫成愈短風險愈高。

The longer the payback period, the riskier the project. Do not write that a shorter payback is riskier.

4B　淨現值Net present value

淨現值等於未來淨現金流入的現值減去最初的支出。一個項目的淨現值反映它對企業的預期價值。單位是元。

NPV is the present value of future net cash inflows, less the initial outlay. It indicates the expected impact of the project on the value of the firm. The unit is dollars.

正數的淨現值：增加企業的價值。增加的數額就是淨現值本身。

A positive NPV is expected to increase the value of the firm. The increase equals the NPV.

淨現值 = 各年現金流入 ÷ (1+i)年 的合計 − 最初支出

NPV = the sum of each year’s cash inflow ÷ (1+i)year, minus the initial outlay

決策規則Decision rule

可同時進行的項目

Projects that can be taken together

中文筆記：淨現值大於或等於零便接受。

English notes: accept the project if NPV is positive.

互相排斥的項目

Mutually exclusive projects

兩個項目的淨現值皆為正數時，選擇淨現值較高者。

When both projects have a positive NPV, accept the one with the highest NPV.

企業的目的

The firm’s goal

The two notes do not use the same cut-off words. The Chinese notes accept NPV greater than or equal to zero. The English notes accept a positive NPV. An NPV of zero does not increase the value of the firm. On a Chinese paper follow “greater than or equal to zero”; on an English paper follow “positive”.

4C　內部報酬率Internal rate of return

內部報酬率是資本項目的淨現值等於零時的貼現率，是一項年利率。單位是 %。

The IRR of a capital project is the discount rate at which the NPV of the project equals zero. It is a measure of the annual percentage yield on the investment. The unit is %.

本章不要求計算內部報酬率。假設：除了初期投資之外，全部現金流量都必須是正數。若投資期中間出現現金流出，假設被違反，可以出現多個內部報酬率。

This chapter does not require the calculation of IRR. Assumption: all cash flows except the initial investment must be positive. If there is a cash outflow during the investment horizon, the assumption is violated and multiple IRRs can exist.

決策規則

Decision rule

只要內部報酬率大於或等於企業所要求的回報率（或資本成本），便接受該項目。

Accept if the IRR is above or equal to the firm’s required rate of return (or cost of capital).

易錯：內部報酬率較高就一定令企業價值增加較多。它只是百分比，忽略投資規模。

Trap: a higher IRR must increase firm value by more. IRR is only a percentage and ignores the scale of the investment.

5.1　回收期Payback period

優點：計算簡單，定義易明

Strength: simple to calculate, and the definition is easy to understand

沒有涉及金錢的時間值，所以計算簡單。小型項目要快決定時（例如更換一件小工具），可以用它節省時間。

It does not involve the time value of money, so it is simple to calculate. For a quick decision on a small project, such as replacing a small tool or tuning up a machine, it saves time.

優點：用現金流量，不用利潤

Strength: cash flows, not accounting profit

現金流量是企業實際收到或付出的金額。

Cash flows are the actual amounts received or paid by the firm.

優點：增加再投資的機會

Strength: more chances to reinvest

回收期短的項目讓企業快點收回投資成本，於是有更多現金再投資。企業有好機會但現金不足時，會選較短的回收期。

A short payback lets the firm recover the investment cost quickly and have more cash for reinvestment. If the firm has good opportunities but insufficient cash, it can choose the shorter payback.

優點：識別高風險的項目

Strength: it helps identify risky projects

經營環境變得快。回收期越長，風險越高，所以企業可以用回收期把高風險項目認出來。

Business conditions change quickly. The longer a project’s payback period, the riskier the project, so the firm can identify risky projects from the payback period.

缺點：不理回收期之後的現金流

Weakness: cash flows after the payback period are ignored

較長回收期的項目可能在回收之後有多很多的現金流，仍會被拒絕。這會做成錯誤決定。

A project with a longer payback may be rejected even if it has much larger cash flows than the others after the payback period.

缺點：不理金錢的時間值

Weakness: time value of money is ignored

兩個項目可以有相同的回收期，但每年現金流的先後不同。回收期分不出來，可能做成錯誤決定。

Two projects can have the same payback period even if the timing of their cash flows differs. Payback cannot tell them apart, and the decision can be wrong.

缺點：標準主觀，也不顯示項目價值

Weakness: the cut-off is subjective, and value is not shown

可接受的最長回收期由管理層訂立。回收期也沒有顯示投資實際帶來的價值。

Management decides the maximum payback period. The method also does not show the financial value of the investment.

5.2　淨現值Net present value

優點：現金流量、全部年份、金錢的時間值

Strengths: cash flows, every year, and the time value of money

它用實際現金流量，不是利潤。所有現金流都計算。金錢的時間值也計算在內。

It uses actual cash flows, not accounting profit. All cash flows are considered. The time value of money is considered.

優點：直接連到企業價值

Strength: it is tied directly to firm value

淨現值以金額表達。企業投資於淨現值為正數的項目，公司價值會按該淨現值增加。這是股東得到的絕對金額。

NPV is in dollars. A project with a positive NPV increases the value of the firm by that amount. It reflects the benefit to shareholders in absolute dollars.

優點：決策標準較客觀

Strength: the criterion is more objective

資本成本可以從資本市場找到。

The cost of capital can be found in the capital market.

缺點：難理解、難計算

Weakness: hard to understand and hard to calculate

計算涉及金錢的時間值和較複雜的公式。

The calculation uses the time value of money and a complicated formula.

缺點：資本成本難估準

Weakness: an accurate cost of capital is hard to set

所要求的回報率或資本成本難以算準。年期愈長，這個問題愈大。

It is difficult to determine an accurate required rate of return or cost of capital. The figure becomes less reliable as the number of years increases.

5.3　內部報酬率Internal rate of return

優點：百分比易明

Strength: a percentage is easy to understand

回報以百分比表示，沒有受過財務訓練的人也較易明白。淨現值則說不出回報率是多少。

The return is a percentage, so people without formal finance training can follow it. NPV does not state the project’s rate of return.

優點：現金流量、全部年份、金錢的時間值、標準較客觀

Strengths: cash flows, every year, time value of money, and a more objective cut-off

它用實際現金流量，考慮所有現金流和金錢的時間值。資本成本可以從資本市場找到。用內部報酬率做決定時，不必把資本成本代進每一年的折現公式。

It uses actual cash flows, considers all cash flows and the time value of money, and the cost of capital can be found in the capital market. Using IRR does not require first putting that cost of capital into the NPV formula.

缺點：難計算，而且可能多於一個答案

Weakness: hard to calculate, and more than one answer may appear

計算要靠假設，也要試誤。中間若有現金流出，可以出現多個內部報酬率。

The calculation needs assumptions and trial and error. A cash outflow during the horizon can produce multiple IRRs.

缺點：不顯示企業價值增加了多少

Weakness: it does not show how much firm value rises

它只顯示回報百分比，忽略投資規模。內部報酬率較高，不等於企業價值增加較多。

It only shows a rate of return and ignores the scale of the investment. A higher IRR does not mean the firm’s value rises by more.

5.4　會計報酬率Accounting rate of return

優點：簡單，而且用現成會計資料

Strengths: simple, and it uses accounting figures already on hand

沒有金錢的時間值，經理較易明白定義。它使用已有的會計資訊。

It does not involve the time value of money, so managers find the result easier to understand. It uses readily available accounting information.

優點：考慮全部回報，並以百分比顯示

Strengths: all returns, shown as a percentage

它考慮整段投資的回報，不像回收期那樣不理回收之後的現金流。它亦給出一個回報百分比。回收期則不顯示投資的財務價值。

It considers all the returns from the investment, while payback ignores cash flows after the payback period. It also gives an idea of the return as a percentage. Payback does not show the financial value of the investment.

缺點：用利潤，不理金錢的時間值，而且折舊方法會影響結果

Weaknesses: profit, no time value of money, and depreciation can change the result

利潤不一定等於企業實際收到或付出的金額。它把今天的一元和將來的一元當成同樣價值。不同的折舊方法可以得出不同的會計報酬率。

Profit does not necessarily equal the cash the firm actually receives or pays. The method treats a dollar now and a dollar in the future as the same value. Different depreciation methods can change the ARR.

中文筆記的優點有一句「考慮所有的現金流量」。會計報酬率的計算基礎仍然是利潤。那一句的意思是整段投資期的回報都納入，不是說它改用現金流量計算。

One Chinese-notes strength says the method considers all cash flows. ARR is still based on profit. That line means the whole life’s returns are included. It does not mean ARR is calculated from cash flows. The English notes say “all the returns.”

5.5　結論不一致時When the methods do not agree

回收期較短，淨現值卻較低

Shorter payback, but a lower NPV

若兩個項目的回收期都短於或等於企業的上限，應選淨現值較高的項目。它令企業價值增加較多。不要因為回收期較短就自動選另一個。

If both projects meet the firm’s maximum payback period, choose the project with the higher NPV. It increases the firm’s value by more. Do not automatically take the shorter payback.

淨現值相對內部報酬率的兩項優勢

Two advantages of NPV over IRR

淨現值直接連到企業價值；內部報酬率不是。淨現值較高，價值就增加較多。內部報酬率較高並不保證價值增加較多，因為它忽略規模。

NPV is directly related to firm value. IRR is not. A higher NPV increases firm value by more. A higher IRR does not guarantee a larger increase, because it ignores scale.

內部報酬率比淨現值更難計算。它要試誤，而且要做假設來簡化計算。

IRR is harder to calculate than NPV. It needs trial and error, and assumptions are made to simplify the calculation.

內部報酬率相對淨現值的兩項優勢

Two advantages of IRR over NPV

百分比較易被沒有受過財務訓練的人理解。淨現值說不出回報率。

A percentage is easier for people without formal finance training. NPV does not tell the rate of return.

筆記的答法：用內部報酬率做決定，不必做淨現值那種複雜的資本成本計算。你仍是把內部報酬率和資本成本比較，只是不必把資本成本代進每一年的折現。

The notes’ line: using IRR for the decision does not require the complicated calculation of the cost of capital which NPV requires. You still compare the IRR with the cost of capital. You do not put that rate into a present-value formula for every year.
