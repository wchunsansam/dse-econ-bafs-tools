12.1 貿易障礙的形式Forms of Trade Barriers

貿易障礙是指以任何形式來限制或打擊國際貿易的政府規例。

A trade barrier refers to any form of government regulation that restricts or discourages international trade.

為保護本地工業而利用經濟政策（如貿易障礙）限制國家之間的貿易，這種行為稱為保護主義。

Trade protectionism refers to the policy of imposing measures to restrict international trade so as to protect a country’s interests.

貿易障礙有許多不同形式，均旨在限制進口量或出口量。

There are many forms of trade barriers and they all serve the purpose of restricting quantities of imports or exports.

形式Form 意思Meaning
關稅（進口稅）Tariff (import duty) 向進口物品徵收的稅。可以是每單位一個固定金額（從量關稅），或按物品價值一個固定百分比（從價關稅）。The tax levied on imported goods: a unit tax (fixed amount per unit of imports) or an ad valorem tax (fixed percentage per unit of imports).
進口配額（非關稅障礙）Import quota (non-tariff barrier) 在一段指定時間內，一國能夠進口某物品的數量上限。The maximum quantity imposed on imports.
禁運（非關稅障礙）Embargo (non-tariff barrier) 在法律上禁止與某指定國家進行貿易：可禁止所有物品，或只禁止指定物品。進口量上限可以是零。A legal prohibition of trade with a particular country: a ban on all goods, or on specific goods. The maximum quantity imported can be zero.
生產津貼（非關稅障礙）Production subsidy (non-tariff barrier) 政府發放的經濟補助，對象是出口商或需要與進口物品競爭的行業生產者，令本地物品價格更具競爭力。Financial aid to exporters or producers in import-competing industries, making domestic prices more competitive.
外匯管制（非關稅障礙）Exchange control (non-tariff barrier) 政府對私人買賣外匯的限制，藉限制可供購買進口物品的外幣數量來限制進口。A government restriction on private transactions of foreign currencies, limiting imports by limiting the foreign currencies available to buy them.

有些國家還會徵收進口附加稅：在現有關稅之上，對進口物品再徵一筆稅。

Some countries also impose an import surcharge: a tax on imported goods on top of an existing tariff.

應試攻略：貿易障礙是政府措施

Exam tip: a trade barrier is a government measure

正確：關稅、配額、禁運、生產津貼、外匯管制、進口附加稅——都是政府出手。

Correct: a tariff, a quota, an embargo, a production subsidy, exchange control and an import surcharge are all government measures.

易錯：把運費、兩國機會成本不同、入口國成本高於貿易比率寫成貿易障礙。那些是成本或比較優勢，不是政府規例。511 已說明運費不是貿易障礙。

Trap: calling transport cost, different opportunity costs, or an importer’s cost being above the terms of trade a trade barrier. Those are costs or comparative advantage, not government regulation. Notes 511 already treat transport cost as not a trade barrier.

社際攤位：學生會怎樣限制校外凍飲House stall: how the student union can restrict drinks from outside

樂澄的社想保護自己的凍飲攤。學生會若規定：校外買入的每杯凍飲要多繳 $2，這像從量關稅。若按杯價加 10%，這像從價關稅。若全日最多只准帶入 20 杯，這像配額。若禁止某間連鎖店的杯子進校園，這像禁運。專線小巴把貨運到校門的車資仍是運費，不是貿易障礙。

Chloe’s house wants to protect its drink stall. If the student union charges $2 on every drink brought in from outside, that is like a unit tax. A 10% charge on the cup price is like an ad valorem tax. A ceiling of 20 outside drinks a day is like a quota. Banning one chain’s cups is like an embargo. The minibus fare to the gate is still transport cost, not a trade barrier.

12.2 自由貿易對小型開放經濟的經濟影響Economic Effects of Free Trade on a Small Open Economy

小型經濟的含義Meaning of a small economy

小型進口開放經濟是指該經濟體系有參與國際貿易，但其進口量只佔國際市場一個極小的比例。

A small open economy is one whose imports only account for a very small proportion of the good in the world market, implying that its change in quantity imported would have an insignificant effect on the world price.

  • 該經濟體系在進口量上的任何改變，甚至完全停止進口，都不會影響該物品的國際價格。它是國際價格的受價者。
  • Its change in quantity imported would have an insignificant effect on the world price. It is a price taker in the world market.
  • 它面對的全球供應曲線呈水平狀：在國際價格下，出口國家能按它的需求供應任何數量。
  • Foreign suppliers are willing to supply any quantities at Pw, so the supply curve turns horizontal after Qs.

應試攻略：小型經濟改進口量，國際價格不變

Exam tip: a small economy does not change the world price

正確：關稅、配額、需求增加，都不會把 Pw 推高或壓低。

Correct: a tariff, a quota or a rise in demand will not raise or lower Pw.

易錯：見到本地價格升到 P1，就把國際價格也寫成升到 P1。

Trap: seeing the domestic price rise to P1 and writing that the world price also rises to P1.

沒有國際貿易的情況Situation without international trade

若一個國家不參與國際貿易，便處於閉關自守的狀態，僅靠自給自足。競爭性市場的均衡由本地需求和本地供應決定。

An economy without international trade is a closed economy. Equilibrium is determined by domestic demand and domestic supply. Domestic consumption is equal to domestic production at Q₀.

自給自足：D 與 S 相交於 (Q₀, P₀)。本地消費 = 本地生產 = Q₀。

Autarky: D and S meet at (Q₀, P₀). Domestic consumption = domestic production = Q₀.

實行自由貿易的情況Situation with free trade

本章假設國際價格（Pw）低於貿易前的均衡價格（P₀）。小型進口國在 Pw 下可購買任何數量，因此自由貿易下的供應曲線 Sf 在 Pw 呈水平。

Free trade refers to international trade without any restrictions imposed by the government. This chapter assumes the world price (Pw) is below the domestic price (P₀): the country is an importing country. A small open economy is a price taker of the world price, so the free-trade supply curve turns horizontal at Pw.

  • 當本地價格低於 Pw：只有生產成本低於或等於本地價格的本地生產者才願意供應。這一段與本地供應曲線最初向右上斜的部分相同。
  • Without trade, domestic supply is an upward-sloping curve. When P < Pw, the supply curve faced by consumers is the same as the domestic supply curve.
  • 當本地價格升至 Pw：該國願意進口。Sf 在 Pw 變成水平線。
  • With trade, foreign suppliers are willing to supply any quantities at Pw. The supply curve turns to become horizontal after Qs (Sf).

若 Pw > P₀，本地生產商會以更高的國際價格出口。本章主線是進口國。

If Pw > P₀, domestic producers export at the higher world price. This chapter’s main line is the importing country.

自由貿易：本地價格降至 Pw。本地消費升至 Qd，本地生產降至 Qs，進口量 = Qd − Qs。

With free trade, the domestic price is Pw instead of P₀. Domestic consumption is at Qd whereas domestic production is at Qs. The difference (Qd − Qs) is imports.

自給自足時：本地消費 = 本地生產。進口國參與貿易時：本地消費 = 本地生產 + 進口數量。

Without trade, domestic consumption = domestic production. With free trade, domestic consumption is at Qd whereas domestic production is at Qs; the difference is imports.

自由貿易前Without trade 自由貿易後With trade 改變Change
本地價格Domestic priceP₀Pw下降falls
本地消費Domestic consumptionQ₀Qd上升rises
本地生產Domestic productionQ₀Qs下降falls
進口數量Imports—Qd − Qs—

12.3 關稅對小型開放經濟的經濟影響Economic Effects of a Tariff on a Small Open Economy

關稅是對進口物品徵收的稅。

A tariff is a tax levied on imported goods.

為簡化分析，假設：受保護的本地工業規模極小，不會牽動其他行業；進口國在國際市場是受價者，能以國際價格進口任何數量。

To simplify: the protected domestic industry is tiny, so it does not affect other industries; the importer is a price taker and can import any quantity at the world price.

關稅對供應曲線的影響Effect of a tariff on the supply curve

徵收關稅（T）後，進口皮鞋的本地價格從 Pw 上升至 Pt，升幅等於關稅稅額：Pt = Pw + T。供應曲線最初向右上斜，價格達到 Pt 時變成水平線 St。

The government imposes a tariff to drive up the price of imports so as to reduce the quantity of imports. After a tariff T, the price of the imported goods increases by the amount of the tariff: Pt = Pw + T. The supply curve faced by consumers is upward sloping initially and becomes horizontal at Pt (St).

  • 在任何低於 Pt 的本地價格：只有生產成本低於或等於 Pt 的本地生產者才願意供應。
  • At any domestic price below Pt, only domestic producers whose costs are lower than or equal to Pt will supply.
  • 當本地價格上升至 Pt：進口國願意進口，因為這價格能彌補進口成本及關稅。
  • When the domestic price reaches Pt, the importer is willing to import, because that price covers the import cost plus the tariff.

關稅：本地價格升至 Pt。本地生產 Q₁→Q₂，本地消費 Q₄→Q₃，進口由 (Q₄−Q₁) 降至 (Q₃−Q₂)。面積 A 是關稅收入。

Comparison with tariff: supply curve St, domestic price Pt, production Q₂, consumption Q₃, imports Q₃ − Q₂, tariff revenue area A (or F in the 512 notes).

政府收入 = 單位關稅 × 進口量。在圖中即 (Pt − Pw) × (Q₃ − Q₂)，亦即面積 A。

Government revenue = unit tariff × import volume. In the diagram that is (Pt − Pw) × (Q₃ − Q₂), which is area A.

小型開放經濟徵收關稅，只會影響物品的進口成本，物品的國際價格不會受到影響。

In a small open economy, a tariff affects only the import cost of the good. The world price is not affected.

A lower tariff

提高從量關稅後，關稅收入不一定上升：單位關稅上升，但進口量下降，乘積可升可跌。本地生產者總收入則因 Pt 與本地產出都上升而必然上升。

Raising a unit tax does not necessarily raise tariff revenue: the unit tax rises but imports fall. Domestic producers’ total revenue must rise, because both Pt and domestic output rise.

應試攻略：進口量是 Qd 與 Qs 之差，不是單一 Q

Exam tip: imports are Qd minus Qs, not a single Q

正確：徵收關稅後進口量 = Q₃ − Q₂。

Correct: imports after the tariff = Q₃ − Q₂.

易錯：把進口量寫成 Q₃，或把本地供應量寫成 (Q₂ − Q₁)（那是增量，不是水平）。

Trap: writing imports as Q₃, or writing domestic quantity supplied as (Q₂ − Q₁) (that is the increase, not the level).

12.4 配額對小型開放經濟的經濟影響Economic Effects of a Quota on a Small Open Economy

進口配額是一種貿易限制，是指在一段指定時間內，一個國家能夠進口的物品數量上限。

An import quota is the maximum quantity imposed on imports. The government restricts the quantity of imports directly by the quota limit.

一如關稅：進口國是小型經濟；實施配額不會影響國際價格；面對的全球供應曲線是水平線。

As with a tariff: the importer is a small economy; the quota does not affect the world price; the world supply curve it faces is horizontal.

進口配額對供應曲線的影響Effect of an import quota on the supply curve

  • 當本地價格低於 Pw：進口國不願意進口；消費者面對的供應曲線與本地供應曲線 S 相同。這是折曲供應曲線 Sq 最初向右上斜的部分。
  • When P < Pw: domestic suppliers are willing to supply as they have a lower cost of production than the exporting country. The supply curve faced by consumers is the same as the domestic supply curve.
  • 當本地價格等於或高於 Pw：願意進口，但進口量被配額限制。Sq 的水平部分代表配額的數量。
  • When P = Pw: the importing country is a price taker. Importers are willing to supply as much as the country would like to import at the world price, so the supply curve becomes horizontal — but the horizontal portion equals the quota limit.
  • 進口量達到配額後，若要買更多，必須以較高價格向本地生產者購買。因此當價格 ≥ Pw，皮鞋供應量 = 本地生產 + 配額量，供應曲線以配額量向右移。實施配額後的供應曲線因此是折曲的。
  • When P > Pw: after the quota limit is reached, if consumers would like to buy more, they have to turn to domestic suppliers. The supply curve faced by consumers becomes upward sloping again, so the supply curve with a quota is kinked.

從量關稅、配額與進口物品的平均質素Improvement in the quality of imports due to tariff and quota

對進口物品徵收從量關稅或實施配額後，進口物品平均質素會改善：

Quality of imports improved after imposing a tariff and a quota:

  • 生產者：進口量下降後，生產者（或進口商）會供應較多高質素進口物品，因為每單位利潤較高，有助維持利潤。
  • Producers supply more high-quality imports: the volume of imports drops, so producers (or importers) supply more high-quality imports, which yield greater profit per unit, so that they can maintain their profit.
  • 消費者：每單位都加上同一筆金額，高質素進口物品的相對價格下降。根據需求定律，消費者會購買相對較多高質素進口物品。
  • Consumers buy more high-quality imports: the percentage increase in price for high-quality imports is smaller than that of low-quality imports, so the relative price of high-quality imports falls. By the law of demand, consumers buy more high-quality imports.

實施貿易障礙的理據Arguments for protectionism

雖然貿易障礙會帶來負面影響，國家仍可能使用它們：

Although efficiency is reduced after imposing trade barriers, there are arguments supporting trade protectionism:

  • 保護新興工業：新型工業需要時間發展，初期限制競爭性物品進口，或可保護它們免受國外激烈競爭。
  • Temporary protection for domestic infant industries: new industries need time to develop. Restricting competing imports early on may shelter them from foreign competition.
  • 促進就業：進口物品變得較昂貴，消費者轉買較多本地物品，或令更多本地勞工獲得聘用。
  • Avoiding job losses and pressure to lower wages, and helping to reduce local unemployment.
  • 確保國防安全：軍事設備和農產品等若過份依賴進口，供應一旦停止便威脅安全，因此或會保護本地生產以達到自給自足。
  • Avoiding the risk of over-depending on foreign countries (e.g. military equipment and farm products).
  • 政府收入：關稅可以成為政府收入來源。
  • Potential source of government revenue (a tariff).

應試攻略：需求上升時，關稅與配額的價格反應不同

Exam tip: demand growth moves price differently under a tariff and a quota

關稅：St 在 Pt 呈水平，需求增加時本地價格可以維持在 Pt（只要仍有進口）。

Tariff: St is horizontal at Pt, so when demand rises the domestic price can stay at Pt (while imports continue).

有效配額：進口量被鎖死，需求增加會把本地價格再推高。消費者通常寧願面對關稅而不是配額。

Effective quota: imports are locked, so a rise in demand pushes the domestic price higher. Consumers usually prefer a tariff to a quota.

梓軒的家辦廠：配額之後改做設計Tsz Hin’s family workshop: design after a quota

梓軒家人在內地為外國牌子車縫校服。進口國突然設配額，件數被鎖死。他們把部分車縫遷到越南，並在香港做紙樣和品牌，用較高單價抵銷件數下降。這不是「認識更多國際組織」的空話，而是轉移工序、市場分散和高增值三條出路同時用。

Tsz Hin’s family sews school uniforms on the Mainland for a foreign brand. An importing country sets a quota, so the piece count is locked. They move some sewing to Vietnam and keep pattern-making and the brand in Hong Kong, using a higher unit price to offset fewer pieces. That is relocating processing, diversifying markets and moving up the value chain at once — not just “joining more organisations”.
