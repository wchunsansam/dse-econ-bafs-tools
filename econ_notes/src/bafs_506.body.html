1　介紹Introduction

風險是指蒙受損失或遭遇不幸的可能性。（但有機會獲利。）

Risk is the variability of outcomes, some of which are unpleasant. (loss or mishap)

括號裡的「但有機會獲利」要留到 2.1。那是投機風險。純風險只有損失或沒有損失。

The bracket “(loss or mishap)” stays with this definition. A chance of gain is speculative risk in 2.1. Pure risk is only a chance of loss or no loss.

風險管理的目的：有助企業識別和評估各種潛在風險，並制定措施，務求盡量減少損失。

The objective of risk management is to help a firm identify and assess its exposure to various types of risk, and develop measures to minimise losses.

不同的企業在日常營運中會面對不同的風險，例如火災、偷竊、員工工傷。

Every business faces different risks in its daily operations, for example fire, theft of cash or property, and employees’ injuries or accidents.

2.1　純風險和投機風險Pure risk and speculative risk

根據風險的性質，與企業相關的常見風險可以這樣分類。

Business risks can be classified into different types according to their nature.

純風險Pure risk

純風險是指只有損失或沒有損失兩種機會的風險。

Pure risk (sickness in an individual) involves the chance of loss only.

例子：火災、僱員身故、企業遭起訴。它包括人身風險、財產風險、責任風險。

Examples: destruction of property by fire, death of key personnel, dishonesty of employees, floods, theft.

因此，減少或消除純風險對企業和社會有好處。

The elimination or reduction of pure risk is beneficial to both business and society.

投機風險Speculative risk

投機風險是指同時有損失和獲利機會的風險。

Speculative risk involves the chance of both loss and gain.

例子：金融投資大多涉及投機風險。

Examples: purchasing shares, developing a new market. Changes in customer taste or technology, a change in the economy, and the entry of competitors are market risk. The introduction of new laws is political risk. A change in the exchange rate is exchange-rate risk.

先問有沒有獲利那一邊Ask whether a gain is possible

只有損失或沒有損失，是純風險。損失和獲利都有可能，是投機風險。

Loss or no loss, and nothing else, is pure risk. A chance of both loss and gain is speculative risk.

投機風險不可保Speculative risk is not insurable

只有純風險可以是可保風險。見到獲利機會，不要寫可保。

Only pure risk can be insurable. If the firm might gain, do not call the risk insurable.

請茶的火災和對面新店Try Tea’s fire and the new shop opposite

屯門請茶鋪面火災：後果是損失，或者這次沒有損失，沒有「火災令請茶獲利」這一邊。這是純風險。對面開了一家新茶店：請茶可能流失顧客，也可能因人流增加而獲利。這是投機風險。

A fire at Try Tea in Tuen Mun ends in a loss, or in no loss. The fire does not create a gain. That is pure risk. A new tea shop opens opposite: Try Tea may lose customers, or it may gain from the extra traffic. That is speculative risk.

2.2　可保風險及不可保風險Insurable risk and non-insurable risk

可保風險是承保人可以承保的風險（賠償損失）。

Insurable risks are those covered and accepted by insurance companies for compensation of losses.

風險必須滿足以下條件，才是可保風險，否則就是不可保風險。前五項是編號條件。可保利益和損失可以被量化在同一頁以星號另列，一樣要成立。

To be insurable, a risk must satisfy all of the following conditions. If it fails one of them, it is a non-insurable risk.

# 條件Condition
1 必須是非投機的。（只有純風險是可保的。）The risk must be non-speculative. (Only pure risk is insurable.)
2 必須由意外引起和無法控制。沒有人能準確預測這些不幸事故何時發生，以及如何發生，而且事故必須不是受保人故意造成的。The risk must be accidental (cannot predict accurately when and how the event will take place) and uncontrollable (cannot be intentionally caused by the insured). The loss is beyond human control.
3 不幸事故發生的機會率是可估計的。保險公司會確保它能在一定誤差範圍內估計機會率，以計算保費和預測索償數目。The probability of the undesirable event must be predictable. The events occur regularly enough for the insurance company to calculate the premium and predict the number of claims based on past statistics.
4 損失不可過於頻繁或過小。假如損失過於頻繁或過小，保險公司的行政費用可能會高於保費收入，令它最終蒙受損失。The loss must not be too frequent or too small. Administration costs may exceed the premium income, so it is not profitable.
5 同時蒙受損失的受保人數量不能太多。如果某事故在同一時間內導致大量索償，保險公司可能無力就所有索償作出賠償。The number of insured suffering losses at the same time must not be too great. Insurance companies may not be able to afford the compensation for the claims.
6 投保人對損失有直接的可保利益。The insured has direct insurable interest in the loss.
7 損失可以被量化。The loss can be measurable in monetary terms.

可保風險的例子Examples of insurable risks

課本 p.184。

Textbook p. 184.

  • 財產風險：財產遭毀壞或盜竊的可能性。
  • Property risks: the possibility that property may be damaged, destroyed or stolen.
  • 人身風險：個人生病、受傷或死亡的可能性。（導致企業損失）
  • Personal risks: the possibility of illness, physical disability, retirement or death of a person. These may lead to a potential loss of profits.
  • 責任風險：企業毀壞他人財產或導致他人受傷，因而在法律上須承擔賠償責任的可能性。
  • Legal liability risks: the possibility that a firm is legally liable to pay compensation for causing damage to people’s property or injury to people.

不可保風險的例子Examples of non-insurable risk

  • 顧客口味／科技變化，令產品不合時宜。
  • Changes in customer tastes or technology, which make the product outdated.
  • 新的立法。例如提高行業標準，令生產成本上升。
  • The introduction of new laws, for example safety standards or environmental protection regulations that may drive up production costs.
  • 天災：突然變化的天氣改變了顧客的習慣。
  • Acts of God: freak weather, such as an extremely cold summer that changes people’s habits.
  • 經濟變化：衰退影響需求。
  • Changes in the economy: a recession that can affect demand.
  • 新的競爭者及產品進入市場。
  • The entry of competitive products and/or new competitors into the market.
請茶每天打爛的杯The cups Try Tea breaks every day

小美說：「杯天天打爛，買保險就好。」這項損失過於頻繁，而且每隻杯的損失過小。行政費用容易高於保費，保險公司不會當它是可保風險。對面新店搶客則是投機風險，第一個條件已經失敗。

Karen says: “Cups break every day, so just insure them.” That loss is too frequent and each cup is too small. Administration costs can exceed the premium, so it is not an insurable risk. The new shop taking customers is speculative, so the first condition already fails.

2.3　風險對企業活動的影響Impact of risk on business activities

  • 人命傷亡：僱員、消費者或其他人生病、受傷以至死亡。
  • Personal injury and loss: illness, injury or death of employees, consumers or other people.
  • 財產責任：財產遭毀壞或盜竊。
  • Loss of property: the property is damaged, destroyed or stolen.
  • 法律責任：企業因毀壞他人財產，或導致僱員、消費者或其他人受傷，而要支付賠償。
  • Legal liability: compensation a firm pays for causing damage to people’s property, or injury to its employees, consumers or other people.
  • 利潤損失：企業由於上述任何損失而失去本來可以賺取的利潤。
  • Loss of profits: profits that would have been earned, lost because of any of the losses above.

3　企業普遍使用的保險產品Types of insurance protection

3.1　汽車保險

為司機和第三者因汽車失竊或發生車禍所導致的損失提供保障。

汽車第三者（責任）保險

當投保人駕駛汽車導致第三者受傷、死亡或蒙受財產損失時，受害者或受損者可從第三者（責任）保險獲得賠償。

綜合保險

保障範圍包括第三者（責任）保險所提供的保障，以及受保人的汽車因意外（例如撞車或失竊）而導致的損失或損毀。

本節的綜合保險寫在汽車保險之下。它保自己的車，同時包括第三者責任。不要把它寫成一份與汽車無關的火險。

3.2　員工誠信保險

僱主因僱員的不誠實行為而損失金錢，便可以從員工誠信保險獲得賠償。

對於僱用職員處理巨額金錢的企業來說，這種保險非常重要，因為職員欺詐或盜竊的機會較大。

3.3　責任保險

當投保的企業對他人造成財產損失或人身傷害而遭到索償時，保險公司便會支付賠償。

公眾責任保險

為企業因其營運導致第三者（公眾／顧客）受傷或蒙受財產損失，而要負上賠償責任的風險提供保障。

如果企業的經營場所經常有大量顧客進出，它們一般都會購買公眾責任保險。

僱員賠償保險

當僱員因工受傷或死亡，僱員賠償保險會就事件招致的醫療費用和賠償金額作出經濟補償。

在香港的僱員賠償條例中，這是強制性的。

3.1　Comprehensive insurance

Comprehensive insurance covers losses such as fire and theft. Examples are fire insurance and burglary insurance.

In this section, comprehensive insurance is the fire-and-theft cover. Motor insurance is the next product, not another name for the same policy.

3.2　Motor insurance

Motor insurance protects drivers and third parties against losses from car thefts and car accidents.

It provides compensation to drivers and third parties who die or suffer injuries, and covers damage to their properties due to the use of the motor vehicle by the insured.

3.3　Fidelity insurance

Fidelity insurance covers losses arising from employees’ dishonesty, such as fraud, forgery and embezzlement.

It is commonly used by banks, loan companies, and other businesses to cover cashiers and other employees who handle company funds.

3.4　Public liability insurance

Public liability insurance covers compensation for third parties’ (the public or customers) injuries inside the company due to the company’s negligence.

It is common for businesses operating in facilities frequently visited by the public (shopping malls, supermarkets, football stadiums, hotels and theatres). For example, a visitor slips on a wet floor and is injured.

3.5　Employees’ compensation insurance

Employees’ compensation insurance covers compensation for employees’ injuries or death at work, for example medical expenses and financial compensation.

It is compulsory in Hong Kong under the Employees’ Compensation Ordinance.

請茶的四張單Four bills at Try Tea

顧客在濕地板滑倒，是公眾責任保險。收銀員偷走舖頭現金，是員工誠信保險。員工搬箱弄傷腰，是僱員賠償保險，香港法例強制要買。請茶的車失竊，走汽車保險裡的綜合保險：保自己的車，也包含第三者責任。

A customer slips on the wet floor: public liability insurance. A cashier steals the cash: fidelity insurance. A staff member hurts their back moving boxes: employees’ compensation insurance, which is compulsory in Hong Kong. The van is stolen: motor insurance, which covers car theft and accidents.

4　風險管理策略（控制風險）Risk management strategies

策略Strategy 迴避程度How far the risk is avoided 在做甚麼What it does
風險迴避Risk avoidance 100% 完全避免損失的風險Eliminating the risk of loss
風險減少Risk reduction 70–90% 減低損失風險Reducing the risk of loss
風險承擔Risk assumption 0%（自己食）0% (the firm bears it) 企業自行承擔Bearing the risk of loss
風險轉移Risk transfer 0%（俾人食）0% (another party bears it) 把風險交給他人承擔Transferring the risk of loss to another party

4.1　風險迴避Risk avoidance

風險迴避是指徹底消除可能導致損失的風險。企業完全不參與可能導致損失的活動，以減低損失頻率。

Take actions to avoid some unpleasant outcomes (completely avoid exposure to the risk). It can be accomplished by not engaging in activities which would cause loss.

例子：企業放棄投資高風險的項目；廠房從地震／海嘯地區搬走；銀行避免給予不能達到信用標準的公司借貸。

Examples: companies give up some high-risk segments, such as staying away from politically unstable regions; factories move a proposed warehouse from an earthquake, tsunami or war-sensitive region; a candy manufacturer eliminate all red colourings so they are not linked to a carcinogenic dye; restaurants stop providing chicken dishes during avian flu; banks avoid lending to firms that cannot meet credit standards.

請茶不在水浸碼頭擺檔Try Tea does not pitch at the flood pier

小新說：「水浸碼頭人流多，擺檔就好。」小麗父母不在那裡擺檔。他們沒有參與這項活動，洪水損失的風險被除掉。這是風險迴避，不是買了保險之後仍去擺檔。

Sam says: “The flood pier is busy, so pitch the stall there.” Eslyn’s parents do not. They never take on that activity, so the flood loss is eliminated. That is risk avoidance. Buying insurance and still pitching the stall is not avoidance.

4.2　風險減少Risk reduction

風險減少是指減少可能導致損失的風險。

Risk reduction means implementing techniques that reduce some risks to an acceptable level.

避免損失Loss prevention

有助避免出現損失，即減低損失頻率／損失嚴重度的措施。

Prevent losses from happening (reducing loss frequency).

  • 在濕滑的地板旁放置警告牌，提醒人們避免滑倒。
  • Install smoke detectors to reduce the risk of loss from fire.
  • 企業鎖起現金，以免失竊。
  • Install anti-theft alarms to reduce the risk of loss from theft.
  • 安裝防盜鐘，以免偷竊。
  • Provide employee education and training, and screen industrial machinery to prevent accidents.
減少損失Loss reduction

有助減低損失頻率／損失嚴重度的措施。不能防止不幸事故的發生，只能在事故發生時減少損失。

Once an undesirable event occurs, reduce the damage (reduce loss severity).

  • 酒店安裝自動灑水系統，以減少因火災引致的損失。
  • Install an automatic sprinkler system to reduce the loss from fire.
  • Provide safety equipment to employees to reduce the loss from accidents.
  • Provide disaster drilling regularly to reduce the loss when a disaster occurs.

中文影印本兩類都在「損失頻率／損失嚴重度」下面畫線，沒有先選邊。英文影印本則把避免損失的線畫在 frequency，把減少損失的線畫在 severity。

On the English photocopy the line under loss prevention is frequency, and the line under loss reduction is severity.

請茶的警告牌和灑水Try Tea’s detector and sprinkler

小美在濕地板旁放警告牌，想減少有人滑倒的次數。這是避免損失，減低損失頻率。鋪後的灑水系統不能阻止每一場火，但火起了可以減少燒毀的範圍。這是減少損失，減低損失嚴重度。兩項都是風險減少，不是風險迴避：店仍然開門。

Karen fits a smoke detector. In these notes that is loss prevention: it reduces how often a fire becomes a loss. The sprinkler does not stop every fire, but it limits the damage. That is loss reduction: lower severity. Both are risk reduction. The shop stays open, so this is not risk avoidance.

4.3　風險承擔（風險自留）Risk assumption / risk retention

企業自行承擔可能導致損失的風險。

Risk assumption (risk retention) can be accomplished by accepting the loss when it occurs.

合適的情況是：保障該風險的成本大於總損失。

It fits when the cost of insuring against the risk is greater than the total losses.

  • 用於輕微損失（小風險）。例子：餐廳刀叉的損壞。
  • For an insignificant loss (a small risk). Example: the loss of forks or plates in a restaurant.
  • 用於很少發生但又嚴重的損失（不能避免或轉移，於是自行承受）。例子：911、海嘯、金融風暴。
  • For an infrequent but significant loss (cannot be avoided or transferred, so it is retained). Examples: 911, Tsunami in 2003, Financial Crisis in 2008.

根據這個方法，企業會預留一筆資金來應付潛在的損失。

One way is to set up a reserve fund to cover the loss.

請茶的碎杯錢Try Tea’s cup fund

請茶每天都有杯碎，買保險的成本高於這些碎杯。小麗父母預留一筆碎杯錢，碎了就用這筆錢補。這是風險承擔。店沒有因此結業，所以不是迴避；也沒有把賠償交給保險公司，所以不是轉移。

Cups break often, and insuring them would cost more than the cups. Eslyn’s parents keep a small reserve and replace a cup from it. That is risk assumption. The shop does not close, so it is not avoidance. An insurer does not pay, so it is not transfer.

4.4　風險轉移Risk transfer

企業可透過簽訂合約或購買保險，把風險轉移給他人。

Risk transfer causes another party to accept the risk, by taking out insurance or making a contract.

購買保險Insurance

最常見的風險轉移方法（可保風險）。可把風險由受保人轉移到保險公司。不幸事故發生後，企業獲得經濟賠償（賠償上限等於保額）。

Insurance is a major type of risk transfer (in the case of insurable risks). The risk moves from the insured (policyholders) to the insurance company (the insurer).

簽訂合約A contract

例如，建築公司可以把危險的工作外判，從而把責任風險轉移給另一家企業。

A construction firm may outsource dangerous work and transfer the liability risk to another firm. Buying a put option is another contractual transfer.

買了保險仍是轉移，不是迴避Insurance is transfer, not avoidance

活動繼續做，損失改由保險公司賠，是風險轉移。完全不做那項活動，才是風險迴避。

The activity continues and another party pays the loss: risk transfer. The activity is not done at all: risk avoidance.

4.5　選擇策略時須注意的事項Points to note when choosing a strategy

（1）迴避是最後選擇(1) Avoidance is the last resort

風險迴避策略只應作為最後選擇。

Risk avoidance should only be used as a last resort.

（2）保留某些風險(2) Retain some risks

企業應保留某些風險。

Firms should retain some risks. Retaining a risk involves an opportunity cost.

（3）在策略之間取得平衡(3) Strike a balance

企業應在不同的策略之間取得平衡。

Firms should strike a balance between different strategies.

小新要結業Sam wants the shop shut

小新說：「有火災風險，請茶應該結業。」結業是風險迴避，只可作最後選擇。請茶可以裝警告牌和灑水（減少）、預留碎杯錢（承擔）、買公眾責任保險和僱員賠償保險（轉移）。四項一起用，才是平衡。

Sam says: “There is a fire risk, so Try Tea should close.” Closing is risk avoidance, and that is the last resort. The shop can fit a smoke detector and a sprinkler (reduction), keep a cup fund (assumption), and buy public liability and employees’ compensation insurance (transfer). Using them together is the balance.
