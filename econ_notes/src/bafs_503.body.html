1　融資方式的分類Classification of sources of financing

三種分類。考試寫某一種融資方法時，三軸都要分得清。

Three categories. In the exam, one financing method must be placed on all three axes.

1.1　短期籌資和長期籌資（時間）Short-term financing and long-term financing (time)

短期籌資是指獲得短期資金的融資方式，所得的資金可供企業使用不足一年。

Short-term financing refers to the sources of financing that provide capital for a period of less than one year.

長期籌資是指獲得長期資金的融資方式，所得的資金可供企業使用一年以上。

Long-term financing refers to the sources of financing that provide capital for a period of more than one year.

1.2　舉債融資和股本融資（資本的提供者）Debt financing and equity financing (fund supplier)

股本融資代表資金由企業東主提供。

Equity financing means that funds are supplied by the owners of the firm.

舉債融資代表資金由企業東主以外的人提供。

Debt financing means that funds are supplied by people other than the owners.

1.3　內部籌資及向外籌資（在哪裡籌集資金）Internal financing and external financing (where the funds are obtained from)

內部籌資代表企業以內部產生的現金流量來應付資金需要。

Internal financing means that funds are obtained from the firm’s internally generated cash flows.

向外籌資代表企業向外界籌集資金。

External financing means that funds are obtained from sources outside the firm.

時間：短／長Time: ST / LT + 提供者：股本／舉債Supplier: equity / debt + 來源：內部／向外Source: internal / external → 一種方法、三個標籤One method, three labels

應試攻略：每一種融資方法可以分類為三種融資方式

Exam tip: each financing method can be classified into these three categories

正確：10 年銀行貸款 = 長期融資 + 舉債融資 + 外界融資。易錯：只寫「貸款＝舉債」就停筆，漏了時間和內外。

Correct: a 10-year bank loan = long-term financing + debt financing + external financing. Trap: stopping at “loan = debt” and leaving out time and internal / external.

請茶裝修貸款Try Tea’s renovation loan

小麗父母要為屯門請茶翻新舖面，向銀行借一筆 10 年期裝修貸款。錢不是股東掏腰包，也不是店裡賺回來的留存利潤，所以三個標籤一齊寫：長期、舉債、向外。

Eslyn’s parents renovate the Tuen Mun Try Tea shop with a 10-year bank loan. The money is not from the owners’ pockets and not from retained profits, so write all three labels: long-term, debt, external.

不要把「股本融資」當成「內部籌資」的另一個名字。發行新普通股是股本、也是向外；只有留存利潤才是內部股本。

Do not treat equity financing as another name for internal financing. Issuing new ordinary shares is equity and external; only retained profits are internal equity.

題 2Q2

小明說：「向股東發行新股就是內部籌資，因為錢來自東主。」錯在哪裡？

Answer: equity ≠ internal; issuing new shares is external financing

2A　短期籌資方式Short-term debt financing

以下都是短期籌資方式。

These are short-term debt financing instruments.

貿易賒帳Trade credit 貿易賒帳

貿易賒帳從供應商中取得。企業延遲支付購貨款項。企業需要在信用期限（又稱賒帳期間）內任何時候結清債務。

It is obtained from its suppliers. The firm can defer payments for its purchases. The firm can settle the payment anytime within the credit period.

茶葉商 30 日帳期30-day credit from the tea supplier

請茶向元朗茶葉批發商入貨，發票寫 30 日內付款。這 30 日店裡可以先賣珍珠奶茶，再才結帳——不是銀行借貸，是供應商給的貿易賒帳。

Try Tea buys from a Yuen Long tea wholesaler on 30-day invoices. For those 30 days the shop can sell cups first and pay later — not a bank loan, but trade credit from the supplier.

銀行短期貸款／銀行透支Short-term bank loans: bank overdraft 銀行透支

銀行短期貸款是向銀行借取的小額款項，用以解決短期的資金需要。例子：銀行透支——企業從往來存款帳戶提取的金額超出其存款額。

An overdraft allows a firm to draw a cheque on its current account for more than it has deposited.

如果企業的規模較大／小或剛開業，銀行可能要求它以資產作抵押品（例如存貨、應收帳款或物業）。

The firm is small or newly established: low creditworthiness, collateral demanded.

週五出糧、週六才旺Payday Friday, busy Saturday

請茶兼職週五出糧，學生客卻週六才湧現。小明建議開一個透支額度，週末銷售入帳後自動填回——這是短期需要，不是買舖。

Try Tea pays part-timers on Friday, but student customers peak on Saturday. Bob suggests an overdraft that fills back when weekend sales hit the account — a short-term need, not a shop purchase.

商業票據Commercial paper 商業票據

商業票據由大型銀行和知名企業所發行的短期債務。

A short-term debt obligation issued by large banks and well-known corporations.

請茶這種新開的地區茶飲店不能當「知名企業」去發行商業票據。不要在小店個案寫商業票據。

A new neighbourhood drinks shop such as Try Tea is not a well-known corporation. Do not write commercial paper for a small-shop case.

應計費用Accrued expenses 應計費用

應計費用是企業在某期間招致，但在期末尚未繳付的費用。企業可以在限期前運用那筆資金，而無須就此支付利息。

Expenses that have been incurred during a period but have not been paid by the end of that period. Usually involve no interest costs.

月尾才出糧Wages paid at month-end

請茶兼職這個月已經上了更，工資卻要月尾才出。這筆應計工資在出糧前仍留在公司戶口，短期可用，通常也不另計利息。

Try Tea’s part-timers have already worked this month, but wages are paid at month-end. Until payday those accrued wages stay in the firm’s account — short-term funds, usually with no extra interest.

出售應收帳款Factoring accounts receivables 出售應收帳款

企業把應收帳款售予財務機構（應收帳款收購商），以提早收回現金。售價通常低於到期日可收取的金額，這是提早收回現金的代價。

Selling accounts receivable at a discount to a financial institution for cash.

學校旅行團賬單A school-tour invoice

小新替請茶接了一單學校旅行團茶點，校務處 60 日後才付款。若下週就要付租金，可以把這張應收帳款折價賣給財務公司，先拿現金——少收的那截就是提早套現的代價。

Sam booked a school-tour drinks order for Try Tea; the office pays in 60 days. If rent is due next week, the receivable can be sold at a discount to a factor for cash now — the discount is the price of getting cash early.

2B　長期舉債融資方式Long-term debt financing

發行債券Issuing bonds 發行債券

有限公司（通常大型企業）可以發行債券來籌集資金。通常到期日大概十年或以上。

Limited companies (especially large corporations) can issue bonds to raise capital. The maturity for bonds is normally 10 years or longer.

銀行長期貸款Long-term bank loans 銀行長期貸款

企業向銀行借取的大額款項，通常用來投資固定資產，例如廠房和機器。

Usually involve a large amount of money for investment in fixed assets.

製冰機五年貸款A five-year loan for the ice machine

請茶要買一台貴的製冰機。小麗不應用週末透支去買會用五年的機器，而應談一筆銀行長期貸款，用機器未來幾年幫店賺錢來還。

Try Tea needs an expensive ice machine. Eslyn should not buy a five-year asset with a weekend overdraft; she should raise a long-term bank loan and repay it from the cash the machine helps the shop earn.

租購及租賃Hire purchase and leasing 租購及租賃

租購：買方租用物品，並同時分期付款購買該物品。在租購期間，買方可以使用物品，但只有在付清全額後，該物品才歸其所有。

Hire purchase is an arrangement by which a buyer rents goods and pays by instalments over a period of time. The buyer can use the goods but does not own them until full repayment is made.

租賃：與租購相似，但企業只可以使用而非擁有該物品。只有某些須要使用昂貴的資產的行業會較常採用。

Leasing is similar to hire purchase, but the firms do not own the goods they lease.

封口機租購Hire-purchase of a sealer

小麗建議請茶用租購買封口機：每月供一筆，機器現在就能封杯，供完才算店的資產。若改租賃，用完年期機器仍不是請茶的。

Eslyn suggests hire-purchase for Try Tea’s cup sealer: pay by instalments, use it now, own it only after the last payment. A lease would let the shop use it without ever owning it.

按揭貸款Mortgage loan 按揭

按揭是以物業作抵押的長期貸款。買家資金不足時，可以用按揭購買物業。

A mortgage is a long-term loan secured by a real property. Property buyers can use a mortgage to purchase properties when they do not have enough funds now.

屯門舖位按揭A mortgage on the Tuen Mun shop

若請茶要買下現時租用的屯門舖，而不是繼續交租，可以用該物業作抵押向銀行做按揭——長期、舉債、向外。

If Try Tea buys the Tuen Mun shop it now rents, it can mortgage that property as security — long-term, debt, external.

A mortgage is secured by real property. Commercial paper is short-term and for large well-known firms; bonds are typically for large limited companies.

2C　長期股本融資；內部及外部籌資Long-term equity financing; internal and external

發行股票Issuing stock 發行股票

有限公司可以透過發行優先股（不是常用）及普通股籌集資金。沒有到期日，因此企業可以永遠使用從發行股票籌集的資金。

Limited companies can issue preference shares and ordinary shares to raise capital. Ordinary share has no maturity date. Thus, capital raised from issuing ordinary shares can be used by the company forever.

留存利潤Retained profits 留存利潤

留存利潤是尚未分派給股東作股息的利潤（企業可以削減分派給股東的股息，從而增加留存利潤）。

Profits that have not been distributed to shareholders as dividends.

Try Tea made a profit this year. Eslyn suggests paying a smaller dividend and using retained profits to open a Yuen Long branch — internal equity, no new shareholders, no bank application.

3A　短期及長期籌資方式的比較Comparison between short-term and long-term financing

比較角度：成本、靈活性、風險。

Comparing aspects: (a) cost of capital, (b) flexibility, (c) refinancing risk.

好處Advantages 壞處Disadvantages
短期籌資Short-term financing

（a）較低成本。短期籌資的流動性比長期籌資的高，所以使用短期籌資的企業可以支付較低的利息。

(a) Less costly. As the liquidity of short-term investment is higher than long-term investment, the required rate of return from investors is lower.

（b）更具靈活性。企業可以根據它的短期需要改變資金的總值。

(b) More flexible. The firms can adjust the amount of capital according to its short-term need.

（c）較大的風險（較不穩定的資金來源）。如果企業採用短期籌資，則須要頻繁地再融資。若依賴短期籌資，須承擔較大／小風險（利率及可使用的資金不確定）。

(c) Higher refinancing risk and thus less stable source of capital. Since frequent refinancing is needed, it is riskier.
長期籌資Long-term financing

（c）提供較穩定的資金來源因此較低風險。企業不用考慮利率及資金將來的變化。

(c) Provides a stable source of capital and thus it is less risky.

（a）較高成本。

(a) More costly.

（b）較低靈活性。即使企業在一年後不再需要資金，在到期日前，它也不可以減少籌集的資金總值。

(b) Less flexible. Even if the firm no longer needs the funds one year later, it cannot reduce the amount until they are due.

應試攻略：短期較便宜，但再融資風險較大

Exam tip: short-term is cheaper, but refinancing risk is higher

  • 問「成本」：短期較低。
  • For cost: short-term is less costly.
  • 問「風險／穩定」：短期較大風險。
  • For risk / stability: short-term has higher refinancing risk.

易錯：把「便宜」寫成「較低風險」。

Trap: writing “cheaper” as if it meant “safer”.

透支買製冰機？An overdraft for the ice machine?

若請茶用透支買製冰機，每幾個月都要再商量額度。萬一銀行收緊、息率急升，機器還在、現金卻斷。這就是短期籌資的再融資風險。

If Try Tea buys the ice machine on overdraft, the limit must be renewed again and again. If the bank tightens or the rate jumps, the machine is still there but the cash stops. That is refinancing risk.

3B　股本及舉債融資的比較Comparison between equity and debt financing

比較角度：控制權、破產風險、成本。

Comparing aspects: (a) dilution effect, (b) bankruptcy risk, (c) cost of capital, (d) tax consideration.

好處Advantages 壞處Disadvantages
股本融資Equity financing

（b）較低的破產風險。沒有到期日（不需償還本金）及財務負擔（不需支付利息）。普通股的股息率不是固定的，企業甚至可以不派發股息。發行新普通股不會為企業帶來財政負擔。

(b) Lower bankruptcy risk. There is no maturity and no interest burden for shares.

（a）削弱東主對企業的控制權。新股東可利用投票權影響公司決策，並增加公司被收購的風險。

(a) More dilution effect. New shareholders have voting rights which dilute existing shareholders’ control and may raise the risk of a takeover.

（c）較高成本。普通股股息不是固定的；若有損失就沒有股息，投資者風險較高，要求的回報也較高（風險和回報呈正向關係）。

(c) More costly. Shareholders may get no dividends and may shoulder capital loss, so their required rate of return — and the cost of equity capital — is higher.
舉債融資Debt financing

（a）不會影響東主對企業的控制權。債權人沒有投票權，不能在股東周年大會上投票。

(a) Less dilution effect. Creditors do not have voting rights, so existing shareholders’ control is unaffected.

（c）較低成本。（1）債券利息是固定的，公司必須發放，投資者風險較低。（2）利息支出是可扣稅的，有助減少舉債融資的成本。

(c) Less costly: the firm must pay periodic fixed interest and principal at maturity, so bondholders’ risk and required return are lower. (d) Interest expenses are tax deductible, so tax expenses can be lowered.

（b）較高的破產風險。無論是否錄得利潤，都必須按時支付利息和償還本金，增加財政負擔；若不能支付，可能會被迫清盤。

(b) Higher bankruptcy risk. Interest must be paid periodically whether the firm makes a profit or not, and the principal must be repaid at maturity.

應試攻略：投票權、利息、扣稅不要混

Exam tip: do not mix voting rights, interest and tax

控制權：股本有投票權會稀釋；舉債沒有投票權。

Control: equity dilutes via voting rights; debt does not.

破產風險：股本無固定利息、無到期日；舉債無論盈虧都要付息還本。

Bankruptcy: equity has no fixed interest and no maturity; debt must pay interest even in a loss year, and repay principal.

成本：股本較高（投資者風險高）；舉債較低，且利息可扣稅。股息不可扣稅。

Cost: equity is more costly; debt is less costly and interest is tax deductible. Dividends are not tax deductible.

新股東要不要進董事會New shareholders on the board?

若請茶為開分店發行新普通股，新股東可以在股東周年大會投票，小麗父母的控制權會被削弱。若改向銀行借五年貸款，銀行沒有投票權，但即使淡季虧損也要照付利息。

If Try Tea issues new ordinary shares to fund a branch, new shareholders can vote at the AGM and Eslyn’s parents lose some control. A five-year bank loan does not give the bank votes, but interest is still due even in a quiet, loss-making season.

3C　內部融資及外部融資的比較Comparison between internal and external financing

比較角度：靈活性、成本、決策自由、集資額（所籌得的金額）。

Comparing aspects: (a) flexibility, (b) cost, (c) freedom in decision making, (d) amount of capital raised.

好處Advantages 壞處Disadvantages
內部籌資Internal financing

（a）更具靈活性。企業可以自行決定何時使用留存利潤，而不須要事前獲取批准。

(a) More flexible. The company can use retained profits anytime without approval from external parties.

（b）較低成本。不牽涉交易成本或利息支出。

(b) Less costly. No transaction costs or interest expense.

（c）決策更自由。使用內部資金不用事先取得外界批准。

(c) Greater freedom in decision making. Internal financing does not require approval from external parties.

（d）集資額有限。企業能動用的留存利潤有限。

(d) The amount of capital raised is limited, constrained by retained profits.
外部籌資External financing

（d）集資額更多。

(d) A greater amount of capital can be raised.

（a）較低靈活性。手續繁複（企業需要得到股東的同意）。

(a) Less flexible. The company has to get approval from stockholders and comply with regulations to issue debt or equity.

（b）較高成本。通常涉及交易成本，例如支付給投資銀行和會計師樓的服務費用。

(b) More costly. Involves transaction costs, e.g. flotation cost.

（c）決策更不自由。須向投資者披露集資目的，以及須事先獲股東批准。

(c) Less freedom in decision making. The firm may have to disclose its purposes for raising funds; new investors may affect decisions.

應試攻略：內部不是「免費」，只是沒有發行費用

Exam tip: internal is not “free money” — it avoids issue costs

正確：內部較靈活、較低交易成本、決策較自由，但留存利潤有限。易錯：寫「內部籌資沒有機會成本」或把發行新股當成內部。

Correct: internal is more flexible, avoids transaction / interest costs, and leaves more freedom, but retained profits are limited. Trap: writing that internal funds have no opportunity cost, or treating a new share issue as internal.

留存利潤不夠開分店Retained profits cannot cover a new branch

請茶戶口裡的留存利潤只夠換封口機，不夠在元朗租舖加裝修。小麗可以先用內部資金換機器；開分店就要向外——借貸或找新股東，並接受審批和披露。

Try Tea’s retained profits can replace the sealer but cannot cover a Yuen Long lease plus fitting-out. Eslyn can fund the machine internally; the branch needs external finance — a loan or new shareholders — with approval and disclosure.

4　選擇融資方法的原則Basic principles for choosing financing methods

4.1　內部籌資 VS 外部籌資Internal financing VS external financing

整體而言，企業應盡量使用內部融資。

A firm should use internal financing as far as possible.

  • 更具靈活性
  • More flexible
  • 較低成本
  • Less costly
  • 決策更自由
  • greater freedom in decision making
  • 不會增加新東主
  • Does not result in additional owners

如果內部融資籌集不足資金，企業應該選擇外部籌資。

If internal financing is not able to generate sufficient funds, firms should turn to external financing methods.

壞處提醒：內部的集資額有限。

Reminder: the amount raised internally is limited.

4.2　股本融資 VS 舉債融資Equity financing VS debt financing

如果破產的風險低，應該使用舉債融資。

If the risk of bankruptcy is low, debt financing should be used.

適合沒有什麼向外融資的企業；較低成本。

Suitable for a firm not using debt financing extensively [risk of bankruptcy is low]. The firm can normally obtain debts at low costs.

如果破產的風險高，應該使用股本融資。

If the risk of bankruptcy is high, equity financing should be used.

適合本來已經債台高築的企業。舉債融資需要支付利息及償還本金；股本融資不會增加財務壓力或破產風險。

Suitable for a firm already borrowing too much [risk of bankruptcy is high]. Debt financing has to pay a high interest cost. Equity financing will not further increase its financial burden or risk of bankruptcy.

4.3　短期籌資 VS 長期籌資Short-term financing VS long-term financing

長期籌資適用於有關固定資產的融資。

Long-term financing should be used to finance non-current assets.

企業不用經常再融資。若用短期籌資購買固定資產，又找不到再融資渠道或融資成本激增，便可能出現財政困難。頻繁地再融資，息率可能會上升。

It is unlikely to generate sufficient cash flows in the short term to pay for the non-current assets.

短期籌資適用於滿足短期資金需要。

Short-term financing should be used to meet short-term needs.

企業一般能在短期內售出存貨套現，所收取的現金可用來償還短期債務。

The inventory is expected to be sold for cash in a short period of time, and the cash receipts can be used to settle the accounts payable. Long-term financing has to pay unnecessary financing costs.

固定資產 → 長期Non-current asset → long-term ／ 存貨／短期需要 → 短期Inventory / ST need → short-term

應試攻略：先內部；再看破產風險；長短要配對

Exam tip: internal first; then bankruptcy risk; then match the term

（1）有留存利潤先用內部。

(1) Use internal financing as far as possible.

（2）負債仍低 → 舉債（便宜、不稀釋）；已經債台高築 → 股本（不再加破產風險）。

(2) Low bankruptcy risk → debt; already borrowing too much → equity.

（3）買機器／物業 → 長期；入貨、出糧空檔 → 短期。不要用透支買舖，也不要用十年貸款付今週牛奶帳。

(3) Non-current assets → long-term; inventory and wage gaps → short-term. Do not buy a shop on overdraft, and do not take a ten-year loan for this week's milk bill.
